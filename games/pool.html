Instructions

Pool is a classic billiards game where players aim to pocket balls using a cue stick. Test your precision, strategy, and skills in this timeless game.

Pool has been a popular game for centuries, originating from cue sports. Its digital adaptation has brought the game to a wider audience, making it accessible to players worldwide.

Focus on your aim and calculate angles for accurate shots. Plan your moves ahead to position the cue ball for your next shot.

Controls

  • Mouse — aim the cue and set power
  • Space — take the shot
  • Q / E — add spin to the cue ball

Teacher Toolkit

Teacher Toolkit for Pool

Pool turns each dialogue choice into a close-reading exercise that spotlights classroom discussion norms, family prompts, and privacy-first play sessions.

Close-reading pause

Project a pivotal decision in Pool and have students cite the exact line that justifies their vote before advancing the story.

Family empathy prompt

Send home a one-sentence journal starter such as "What would our family choose in Pool, and why?" so caregivers can join the conversation.

Privacy-first storytelling

Pool runs fully in the browser with no logins; remind learners to close the tab when finished and avoid sharing screenshots that might reveal classmates’ choices.

Family & Classroom FAQ

Pool Classroom FAQ

Answer the top questions families and admins raise about using Pool during instruction.

How can Pool support literacy lessons?

Treat every branch in Pool as a text-dependent question. Students must cite dialogue or codex entries before the class votes on the next move.

What family prompt works with Pool?

Send home a "What would we choose?" scenario so caregivers can discuss empathy, stakes, and character motivation together.

Is Pool safe for classroom devices?

Pool plays entirely in-browser with no accounts. Remind students not to share screenshots that include classmates’ names and to close the tab when done.
