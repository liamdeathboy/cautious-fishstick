Instructions

Pool is a classic billiards game where players aim to pocket balls using a cue stick. Test your precision, strategy, and skills in this timeless game.

Pool has been a popular game for centuries, originating from cue sports. Its digital adaptation has brought the game to a wider audience, making it accessible to players worldwide.

Focus on your aim and calculate angles for accurate shots. Plan your moves ahead to position the cue ball for your next shot.

Controls

  • Mouse — aim the cue and set power
  • Space — take the shot
  • Q / E — add spin to the cue ball

Tips to Master Pool

Build your rhythm, practice short sessions, and keep refining your strategy to stay ahead.
