How does Antbuster support instruction?

Students analyze resource spikes and bottlenecks in Antbuster, then defend which upgrades or defenses to prioritize—a perfect formative assessment.

How can families join in without extra accounts?

Send home a "What would you build next?" screenshot so caregivers can compare strategies using the same privacy-friendly browser version.

Does Antbuster protect student privacy?

Antbuster plays locally in the browser, so no usernames or birthdates are collected. Remind students not to share save files in public chats.
