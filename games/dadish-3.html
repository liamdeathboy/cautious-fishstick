Instructions

Dadish 3 brings fresh zones and trickier hazards to the series. Reach the end flag on each stage to rescue the kids.

Expect longer routes, tighter jumps, and more moving threats. Slow down, observe patterns, and take clean jumps.

Use retries to learn the layout and spot the safest landing path.

Controls

  • Arrow Keys / A-D — move left and right
  • Up Arrow / Space — jump
  • R — restart the level

Tips to Master Dadish 3

Take a quick scan of the whole level before moving so you know where to pause.

Long Routes

Split long paths into checkpoints so you can reset your timing after each safe landing.

Hazard Timing

Move right after a hazard resets to give yourself the widest timing window.

Family & Classroom FAQ

Dadish 3 Classroom FAQ

Answer the top questions families and admins raise about using Dadish 3 during instruction.

How can Dadish 3 support resilience lessons?

Have learners track retries and reflect on how practice improved their timing.

What family prompt works with Dadish 3?

Ask which level felt trickiest and how many attempts it took to clear it.

Is Dadish 3 safe for classroom devices?

Dadish 3 runs in-browser with no accounts. Remind students to close the tab when finished and avoid sharing personal info.
