Instructions

Build speed and momentum through each level to smash into enemies and destructible scenery, since combo score keeps climbing as long as hits and collectibles keep landing without a gap; stopping or taking damage breaks the chain.

Use the taunt to strike a quick pose that can disrupt an enemy attack or parry an incoming projectile when timed correctly, turning what would be a hit into an opening to keep the combo alive instead.

Reaching Pillar John at the end of a level triggers an escape sequence: the level layout effectively reverses and Pizza Face chases you back toward the entrance, so knowing the level's layout from the forward run directly determines how clean the escape run goes.

Controls

  • Arrow keys — move, crouch, and aim jumps

  • X — dash, shoulder bash, and break blocks

Tips to Master Pizza Tower

Because the escape sequence sends you back through the same level at high speed with Pizza Face in pursuit, the layout knowledge built during the forward combo run is what makes the escape survivable — a level cleared slowly and carefully still leaves the escape run just as dangerous if the layout was never actually learned.

Taunting is a tool for keeping combos alive, not just style

Since a well-timed taunt parries an incoming attack instead of taking a hit, using it right before contact with a projectile or an attacking enemy preserves a combo chain that a normal hit would have ended — treating taunt purely as a cosmetic flourish misses its actual defensive function.

Momentum compounds, stopping resets it

Because speed feeds directly into how effectively enemies and scenery get smashed through, slowing down to carefully approach an obstacle usually costs more combo potential than committing to full speed and adjusting the path around what is ahead — Pizza Tower's levels are built to reward continuous motion over cautious platforming.
