Instructions

Pizza Tower is a lightning-fast platformer where you sprint, slam, and wall climb to stop the Pepperman. Score big by keeping combos alive!

Inspired by classic 90s platformers, Pizza Tower features expressive animation, secrets in every level, and an energetic soundtrack.

Chain moves together for huge scores: shoulder bash to keep momentum, dive to break blocks, and grab enemies for style points.

Controls

  • Arrow keys — move, crouch, and aim jumps
  • Z — jump and wall climb
  • X — dash, shoulder bash, and break blocks
  • C — grab enemies and interact

Family & Classroom FAQ

Pizza Tower Classroom FAQ

Clarify how Pizza Tower boosts STEM thinking while keeping privacy in check.

How does Pizza Tower support science standards?

Use Pizza Tower to explain momentum, friction, and cause/effect when turns go well or poorly.

Any family tie-ins for Pizza Tower?

Families can hold a "calmest hands" contest to see who keeps steady control the longest, then discuss strategies.

Is Pizza Tower safe to run on school laptops?

Yes—Pizza Tower loads locally and stores nothing beyond basic progress. A screenshot of the final score is all that’s needed.
