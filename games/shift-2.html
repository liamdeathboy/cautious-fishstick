Instructions

Shift 2 continues the original's core trick: at any point, you can flip the entire level's colors, and what was solid ground in one version becomes empty background in the other, along with hazards that only exist in one state.

Because the flip affects the whole screen at once, most puzzles are solved by timing exactly when to shift — flipping mid-jump to land on a platform that wasn't there a moment ago, or flipping to make a spike pit briefly harmless.

This sequel builds on the original with new elements layered onto the same flipping mechanic, so puzzles tend to combine the shift with additional timing or movement challenges rather than relying on the flip alone.

Controls

  • Arrow keys or WASD — move and jump
  • X or Space — shift between the two versions of the level

Tips to Master Shift 2

Look at both versions of a room before committing to a path — a route that looks blocked in one state might be completely open in the other.

Shift mid-air, not just on the ground

Timing a flip while you're already jumping often opens up a platform to land on that wasn't available when you jumped.

Treat hazards as state-dependent, not fixed

A spike or gap that looks dangerous in one version may be completely absent in the other, so don't assume a hazard is permanent before checking both states.
