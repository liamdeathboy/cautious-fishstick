How to Play Tetris?

Use the arrow keys to move and rotate the blocks. Arrange them to form complete lines, which will then disappear. Try to clear as many lines as possible!

Who Created Tetris?

Tetris was created by Alexey Pajitnov, a Russian software engineer, in 1984. It has since become one of the most popular and enduring games of all time.

Is Tetris Free and Unblocked?

Yes, Tetris is free to play and can be accessed on various platforms. Enjoy the classic puzzle game anytime, anywhere.

Teacher Toolkit

Teacher Toolkit for Tetris

Tetris doubles as a logic lab—ideal for bell ringers that surface problem-solving wins, family challenges, and privacy reminders.

Visible thinking huddle

Ask learners to narrate two moves ahead in Tetris, highlighting math vocabulary like "combine" and "double".

Family puzzler

Print or screenshot a tricky board from Tetris and invite families to predict the next best move at home.

Privacy in practice

Tetris requires zero logins. Reinforce that students should only share puzzles, not personal windows or tabs.

Family & Classroom FAQ

Tetris Classroom FAQ

Clarify how Tetris supports logic practice, family outreach, and privacy.

What classroom benefit does Tetris provide?

Tetris lets students test hypotheses quickly—perfect for math talk routines and error analysis.

How can families engage with Tetris?

Send home a weekly screenshot and ask families to jot the first two moves they would try, then compare notes in class.

Does Tetris keep student information private?

Yes. Tetris runs offline-friendly in the browser with no accounts or tracking beyond basic analytics.
