Family & Classroom FAQ

Riddle School Games Classroom FAQ

Answer the top questions families and admins raise about using Riddle School Games during instruction.

How can Riddle School Games support literacy lessons?

Treat every branch in Riddle School Games as a text-dependent question. Students must cite dialogue or codex entries before the class votes on the next move.

What family prompt works with Riddle School Games?

Send home a "What would we choose?" scenario so caregivers can discuss empathy, stakes, and character motivation together.

Is Riddle School Games safe for classroom devices?

Riddle School Games plays entirely in-browser with no accounts. Remind students not to share screenshots that include classmates’ names and to close the tab when done.
