Ace Gangster Classroom FAQ

Answer the top questions families and admins raise about using Ace Gangster during instruction.

How can Ace Gangster support literacy lessons?

Treat every branch in Ace Gangster as a text-dependent question. Students must cite dialogue or codex entries before the class votes on the next move.

What family prompt works with Ace Gangster?

Send home a "What would we choose?" scenario so caregivers can discuss empathy, stakes, and character motivation together.

Is Ace Gangster safe for classroom devices?

Ace Gangster plays entirely in-browser with no accounts. Remind students not to share screenshots that include classmates’ names and to close the tab when done.
