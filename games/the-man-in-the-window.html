Instructions

The Man from the Window is a narrative horror game told through short, tense chapters. Interact with the environment as prompted and pay attention to the story being told around you.

Reacting correctly to the tapping and other warning cues matters — the game telegraphs danger through sound and small visual changes.

Take chapters slowly. The story rewards attentiveness more than fast reflexes.

The Man from the Window Classroom FAQ

How can The Man from the Window support literacy lessons?

Treat every branch in The Man from the Window as a text-dependent question. Students must cite dialogue or codex entries before the class votes on the next move.

What family prompt works with The Man from the Window?

Send home a "What would we choose?" scenario so caregivers can discuss empathy, stakes, and character motivation together.

Is The Man from the Window safe for classroom devices?

The Man from the Window plays entirely in-browser with no accounts. Remind students not to share screenshots that include classmates’ names and to close the tab when done.
