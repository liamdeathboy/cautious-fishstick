Instructions

Player 1 moves with W, A, S, and D and attacks using J, K, L, U, I, and O for different attack types, while Player 2 moves with the arrow keys and attacks using 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6; an alternate scheme also lets a single player move with arrow keys, attack with X, and defend with C.

Training Mode teaches the full control scheme using familiar characters like Master Roshi and King Kai, making it the recommended starting point before jumping into Story Mode, which replays major Dragon Ball Z events as a series of fights.

Because each player has multiple distinct attack buttons rather than a single strike input, mixing which attack button is pressed rather than repeating the same one is necessary to open up an opponent who has learned to block a single repeated move.

Tips to Master DBZ Devolution

Since both players have several distinct attack inputs available at once, relying on a single button throughout a fight makes an opponent's blocking pattern trivial to time correctly — varying which attack comes next is what actually creates openings against a defending opponent.

Training Mode is worth completing before Story Mode

Because the control scheme spans several attack buttons per player rather than a simple two- or three-button layout, going through Training Mode's guided practice with Master Roshi and King Kai builds the input familiarity that Story Mode's actual fights assume you already have.

About DBZ Devolution

DBZ Devolution is a free, fan-made 2D fighting game based on Dragon Ball Z. It brings the anime's famous fighters, including Goku, Vegeta, Piccolo, Frieza and Cell, to the browser in a fast, sprite-based fighter where you can fly, fire ki blasts, charge energy and transform mid-fight.

You can fight the computer or a friend on the same keyboard, which makes it one of the most popular two-player games for school breaks. Story Mode walks through major arcs of the series as a string of battles, while Training Mode teaches the controls step by step. Because it is a fan project rather than an official Bandai Namco release, character choices and moves reflect the fan developer's interpretation of the show.

DBZ Devolution FAQ

Can two people play DBZ Devolution on one computer?

Yes. Player 1 uses W A S D and the J K L U I O keys, and Player 2 uses the arrow keys and the number keys 1 to 6.

Is DBZ Devolution an official Dragon Ball game?

No. It is an unofficial fan game made as a tribute to Dragon Ball Z, which is why it is free to play in a browser.

What is the best way to learn DBZ Devolution?

Start with Training Mode. Each fighter has several attack buttons, and practising them there makes Story Mode and two-player matches much easier.
