Family & Classroom FAQ

Snailiad Classroom FAQ

Answer the top questions families and admins raise about using Snailiad during instruction.

How can Snailiad support literacy lessons?

Treat every branch in Snailiad as a text-dependent question. Students must cite dialogue or codex entries before the class votes on the next move.

What family prompt works with Snailiad?

Send home a "What would we choose?" scenario so caregivers can discuss empathy, stakes, and character motivation together.

Is Snailiad safe for classroom devices?

Snailiad plays entirely in-browser with no accounts. Remind students not to share screenshots that include classmates’ names and to close the tab when done.
