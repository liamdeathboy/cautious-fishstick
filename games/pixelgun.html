Teacher Toolkit for Pixel Gun

Pixel Gun turns each dialogue choice into a close-reading exercise that spotlights classroom discussion norms, family prompts, and privacy-first play sessions.

Close-reading pause

Project a pivotal decision in Pixel Gun and have students cite the exact line that justifies their vote before advancing the story.

Family empathy prompt

Send home a one-sentence journal starter such as "What would our family choose in Pixel Gun, and why?" so caregivers can join the conversation.

Privacy-first storytelling

Pixel Gun runs fully in the browser with no logins; remind learners to close the tab when finished and avoid sharing screenshots that might reveal classmates’ choices.

Family & Classroom FAQ

Pixel Gun Classroom FAQ

Answer the top questions families and admins raise about using Pixel Gun during instruction.

How can Pixel Gun support literacy lessons?

Treat every branch in Pixel Gun as a text-dependent question. Students must cite dialogue or codex entries before the class votes on the next move.

What family prompt works with Pixel Gun?

Send home a "What would we choose?" scenario so caregivers can discuss empathy, stakes, and character motivation together.

Is Pixel Gun safe for classroom devices?

Pixel Gun plays entirely in-browser with no accounts. Remind students not to share screenshots that include classmates’ names and to close the tab when done.
