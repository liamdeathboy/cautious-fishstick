Instructions

Move with WASD, aim with the mouse, and left-click to fire your currently equipped weapon; switch between up to six carried weapons using the 1 through 6 number keys or by scrolling the mouse wheel.

Because weapon types span very different categories — hitscan guns, flamethrowers with area coverage, spellbooks with special effects — matching the currently equipped weapon to the specific range and situation of an encounter matters more than defaulting to one favorite weapon for every fight.

Switching weapons takes a brief moment during which you cannot fire, so making the swap before an engagement starts (rather than mid-fight while already taking damage) avoids being caught defenseless during the switch itself.

Tips to Master Pixel Gun

Because different weapon categories excel at different ranges and against different numbers of opponents, carrying six weapons but only ever using one defeats the purpose of the loadout system — switching based on the specific encounter (a flamethrower against a group, a sniper rifle at range) uses the mechanic as intended.

Weapon swaps have a vulnerability window

Since switching weapons is not instantaneous, initiating a swap in the middle of an active exchange of fire leaves a brief window where you cannot shoot back — anticipating an upcoming engagement and swapping beforehand avoids that vulnerability entirely.

Number keys are faster than scrolling under pressure

Because the number keys jump directly to a specific weapon slot while scrolling cycles through slots sequentially, memorizing which number corresponds to which weapon allows a faster, more deliberate swap in a tense moment than scrolling and hoping to land on the right weapon.
