Instructions

Thrust with W, Z or the up arrow and turn with A and D or the arrow keys; S brakes with reverse thrust. The craft handles differently depending on where it is - in open space the direction keys rotate you and thrust pushes you along your facing, while standing on a planet's surface the same keys run and jump instead.

Your goal is to find planets and complete them by charging up their sectors. Each planet you finish rewards you with a companion sprite that orbits you from then on, and those companions are not decoration - they feed you energy and fight for you, which is what makes the back half of a run survivable.

Energy is the resource everything runs on and thrusting spends it. The game's own advice is to find energy mass to absorb or fly near a completed planet to recharge, so the loop is to build up a small network of finished planets early and then use them as refuelling points while you push further out into unexplored space.

Controls

  • W / Z / Up — thrust forward, and jump when standing on a planet
  • A / Q / Left — rotate left in flight, strafe left on a surface
  • D / Right — rotate right in flight, strafe right on a surface
  • S / Down — reverse thrust to brake

Tips to Master Space Garden

The 2021 contest was the competition's tenth year and drew 223 entries on the theme of Space. Every one had to fit in 13,312 zipped bytes with no CDN, no image files and no audio files, which is why the intro screen pauses to tell you all sounds have been rendered - the entire soundtrack is generated in your browser at load time. Taking first across that field is the strongest credential a 13KB game holds.

The red drain your energy, and you cannot shoot them

Red harvesters guard the planets and steal energy from you when you get close, and the game is explicit that they can only be killed by planet sprites - the companions you earn by completing planets. Until you have some, the correct answer to a harvester is distance, not aggression. Giant drones show up later and attack the planets you have already finished, so a completed system still needs defending.

Read the HUD before you burn fuel

The display tracks energy remaining, a circular indicator pointing at nearby planets, and running totals of planets discovered against planets completed. Because thrust is the main energy cost, checking the planet indicator before committing to a direction is the difference between arriving somewhere with enough charge to do the work and drifting into empty space with nothing left to recharge from.
