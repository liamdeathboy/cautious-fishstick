Jelly Matches Classroom FAQ

Answer top questions about using Jelly Matches for numeracy and safe play.

How does Jelly Matches reinforce math standards?

Each turn in Jelly Matches requires composing and decomposing numbers, which mirrors the strategies from our math block.

What at-home challenge fits Jelly Matches?

Families can estimate how many steps remain to hit a target score, then compare to the student’s prediction.

Is Jelly Matches private enough for school-issued devices?

Jelly Matches runs without sign-ins, so no personal data leaves the Chromebook. Just remind students to share boards, not browser tabs.
