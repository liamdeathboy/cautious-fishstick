Instructions

Left-click on a chain to cut it, releasing whatever it was holding and letting gravity and the level's other mechanisms take over from there; most puzzles are solved through the order chains get cut rather than any direct control over the balls themselves.

Red balls have to land in red amphorae and green balls in green amphorae specifically, so a solution that gets both balls moving but crosses their destinations does not clear the level even if both balls end up somewhere.

Mechanisms like catapults, ramps, and moving platforms interact with the balls passively once triggered, so timing which chain to cut and in what order determines whether a ball rolls, bounces, or gets launched toward its matching amphora.

Controls

  • Mouse — click to cut chains and interact with level objects
  • R — restart the level to try a new cut order

Tips to Master Civiballs 1

Because cutting one chain can set off a sequence that depends on a previous cut already having happened, cutting chains in the wrong order can send a ball down a path meant for after another mechanism has already fired — planning the full sequence before cutting the first chain avoids sending a ball prematurely down an unprepared path.

Color matching is a hard requirement, not a scoring bonus

Since red balls must land specifically in red amphorae and green in green, a solution that successfully moves both balls to amphorae but swaps their intended colors does not complete the level — tracking which ball is heading toward which amphora throughout a solution matters as much as getting both balls moving at all.

Mechanisms only do what a single trigger allows

Because catapults, ramps, and platforms activate from a single chain cut and then behave passively afterward, a mechanism cannot be redirected once triggered — this makes the timing of the cut that activates each mechanism the actual decision point, since the mechanism's own behavior afterward cannot be adjusted mid-motion.
