Instructions

Crush the Castle hands you a catapult facing a castle built from blocks, with enemies stationed somewhere inside the structure. Your job is to aim and fire projectiles that bring down enough of the building to crush or expose every defender, using real physics for how the structure collapses.

Each level gives a limited number of shots, so a wasted shot that clips the edge of a wall without causing real structural damage can cost you the level even if your aim was close. Later levels introduce different projectile types, some of which explode, split, or behave differently on impact.

Reading how a structure is likely to fall matters more than raw power — a shot that knocks out a key support can bring down an entire tower, while a similar shot elsewhere might barely dent the wall.

Controls

  • Mouse drag — aim and set launch power on the catapult
  • Mouse release — fire the loaded projectile

Tips to Master Crush the Castle

Target supports, not surfaces. A shot aimed at the base of a tower or wall is far more likely to bring the whole structure down than one aimed at the middle.

Save special projectiles for tough structures

Since explosive or splitting projectile types are limited, hold them for the sections of a castle that ordinary shots clearly won't collapse on their own.

Watch a shot fully play out before adjusting

Because physics-based destruction can cascade after impact, wait to see how a hit settles before assuming you've missed — a shot that looks like it missed can still trigger a collapse a second later.
