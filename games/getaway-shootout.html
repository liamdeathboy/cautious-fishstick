Getaway Shootout Classroom FAQ

Answer the top questions families and admins raise about using Getaway Shootout during instruction.

How can Getaway Shootout support literacy lessons?

Treat every branch in Getaway Shootout as a text-dependent question. Students must cite dialogue or codex entries before the class votes on the next move.

What family prompt works with Getaway Shootout?

Send home a "What would we choose?" scenario so caregivers can discuss empathy, stakes, and character motivation together.

Is Getaway Shootout safe for classroom devices?

Getaway Shootout plays entirely in-browser with no accounts. Remind students not to share screenshots that include classmates’ names and to close the tab when done.
