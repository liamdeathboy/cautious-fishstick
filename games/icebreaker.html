Instructions

Icebreaker casts you with a crew of Viking warriors aboard a longship pushing through frozen waters, armed with hammers and shields against whatever the icy sea puts in their path.

As with most single-ship action games, the pressure comes from both sides at once — threats from the water and obstacles from the ice itself — so keeping the crew's attention on the right threat at the right moment is the core challenge.

Controls

  • Arrow keys or WASD — move / steer
  • Space or mouse click — attack with your weapon

Tips to Master Icebreaker

Deal with the closest threat first. A ship under attack from multiple directions loses more from a slow reaction than from a slightly wrong priority.

Watch the ice, not just the enemies

Because the setting is built around a frozen sea, obstacles in the ice itself can matter as much as anything actively attacking your crew.

Keep your shield side facing danger

Positioning matters in a crew-based brawler — facing threats with your guard up costs less than getting caught from an unprotected angle.
