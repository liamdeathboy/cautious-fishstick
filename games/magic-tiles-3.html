Instructions

Magic Tiles 3 challenges you to tap the tiles as they scroll down the screen. Miss a tile and the song ends.

Stay focused on the beat, and use the rhythm to keep your timing steady.

Complete songs to unlock more tracks and tougher patterns.

Controls

  • Mouse / Touch — tap the tiles as they reach the line
  • Hold tiles — press and hold for long notes
  • Pause menu — adjust audio or restart

Tips to Master Magic Tiles 3

Keep your eyes near the hit line and let the rhythm guide your taps.

Combo Focus

Maintain a steady combo to boost your score and confidence.

Pattern Reading

Look ahead to prepare for fast runs and double taps.
