Instructions

Move left and right to steer, and press Up or Space to jump gaps in the tunnel floor; running onto a side wall rotates the entire 3D tunnel so that wall becomes the new floor, which is how the game turns corners that would otherwise be impossible to run through.

Choose Explore Mode to progress through a galaxy map of discrete levels, unlocking new areas and characters as you clear them, or choose Infinite Mode for a single endless run with no fixed end point.

Press R to reset a level instantly after a fall rather than waiting through a respawn animation, and press P to pause — useful since Explore Mode levels can require several attempts to clear a specific gap or rotation.

Controls

  • Arrow keys — guide the runner through tunnels
  • Space — select alternate characters
  • Shift — toggle speed boost

Tips to Master Run 3

A quick tap on the jump key clears small gaps without overshooting onto the next unstable section, while holding it longer sends the runner higher and farther for wide breaks in the tunnel — using a full hold on every gap overshoots the small ones and can run the character straight off the far edge.

Wall rotation is a navigation tool, not just visual flair

Because running onto a wall makes it the new floor, tight tunnel forks are solved by deliberately steering onto a side wall rather than trying to jump across a gap that only makes sense from the current orientation — reading which wall to commit to ahead of time avoids a late, panicked rotation mid-jump.

Infinite Mode punishes memorized routes less than Explore Mode rewards them

Since Explore Mode levels are fixed and repeatable, memorizing a specific level's gap and rotation sequence genuinely pays off on retries, while Infinite Mode generates new sections continuously, so success there depends on reading the tunnel ahead in real time rather than recalling a pattern.
