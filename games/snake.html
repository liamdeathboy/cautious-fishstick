Instructions

Use the arrow keys to steer the snake; it moves continuously in the last direction pressed, so a direction key only needs to be tapped once per turn rather than held down.

Eating a piece of food adds a segment to the snake's length and spawns a new piece of food elsewhere on the grid, so each successful bite makes the snake longer and correspondingly harder to maneuver through the same amount of space.

The run ends immediately the moment the snake's head touches a wall or any part of its own body, so a route that was safe when the snake was short can become a dead end once enough segments have been added.

Controls

  • Arrow keys — change direction instantly
  • Space — pause the game
  • M — toggle sound

Tips to Master Snake

Because every piece of food eaten adds a permanent segment, a movement pattern that comfortably cleared the board early in a run can trap the snake against its own tail later on — planning routes with the snake's current length in mind, not just its length a few moves ago, avoids self-inflicted dead ends.

Turning early beats turning at the last moment

Since the snake moves continuously and only changes direction on a keypress, waiting until the head is directly next to a wall or the snake's own body to turn leaves no margin for error — queuing a turn slightly before it is strictly necessary gives more room to correct if the turn does not clear the hazard as expected.

Open space should be preserved deliberately

Because the snake's own body increasingly fills the available grid as it grows, deliberately looping through open areas rather than winding tightly back and forth keeps more usable space available for later in the run, when a longer snake needs more room to maneuver safely.
