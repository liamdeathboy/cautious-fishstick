How to Play Snake?

Use the arrow keys to control the snake's direction. Collect food to grow longer, but avoid hitting walls or your own tail!

Who Created Snake?

Snake is a timeless classic that originated in the 1970s and became popular on Nokia phones in the 1990s. It remains a favorite among retro game enthusiasts.

Is Snake Free and Unblocked?

Yes, Snake is free to play and unblocked. Enjoy this retro classic anytime, anywhere.
