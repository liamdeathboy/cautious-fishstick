Instructions

Swords and Sandals is built around growing a single gladiator over a series of arena fights — between battles you spend earnings on better weapons and armor and assign stat points to strength, dexterity, vitality, and similar attributes.

Fights themselves play out with your gladiator attacking, blocking, and dodging based on the stats and gear you've built, so a fight going badly is usually a sign to reconsider your build rather than just fight harder with the same setup.

Progressing through tougher opponents and ranks is a long-term investment — a gladiator built with a scattered mix of stats tends to underperform compared to one built with a clear, consistent strategy in mind.

Controls

  • Mouse — navigate menus, buy equipment, and assign stat points
  • Mouse click — choose actions during arena fights

Tips to Master Swords and Sandals

Commit to a build rather than spreading stat points evenly. A gladiator focused on a couple of strong attributes usually outperforms one that's mediocre at everything.

Match your gear to your stat build

Heavy armor and weapons only help if your strength and related stats can actually use them effectively, so buying the most expensive gear isn't automatically the best choice.

Fight opponents near your own rank first

Taking on fights that match your current strength builds up earnings and experience more reliably than reaching for matches well above your gladiator's level.
