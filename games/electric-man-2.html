Instructions

Use the left and right arrow keys to move, and the down arrow to dodge or cartwheel out of a dangerous position; A, S, and D perform standard punch, kick, and grab attacks that cost nothing extra to throw.

Q, W, and E trigger slow-motion punch, kick, and throw attacks respectively, which hit significantly harder than standard moves but require at least one battery point to use — battery points are a limited resource, not something available on every attack.

Because slow-motion attacks are gated behind battery availability, mixing standard attacks (free) with slow-motion attacks (battery-gated) based on current battery count matters more than always reaching for the strongest available move regardless of resource cost.

Tips to Master Electric Man 2

Because Q, W, and E moves require at least one battery point, using them the instant they are available without considering the current fight's pacing can leave no battery reserve for a moment later in the fight where a slow-motion attack would matter more — treating battery as a limited resource to spend deliberately beats using it reflexively.

Precision beats button mashing directly

Since the game is built to reward precise, deliberate attacks over rapid mashing, alternating punch, kick, and grab based on an opponent's current opening rather than repeating one move rapidly lands more hits and takes less retaliatory damage over the course of a fight.

Dodging resets a bad position without spending resources

Because the down-arrow dodge or cartwheel does not cost a battery point, using it liberally to escape a cornered or disadvantageous position is free defensive value compared to trying to fight out of a bad spot with standard attacks that leave you exposed to a counter.
