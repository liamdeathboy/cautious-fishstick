Instructions

BuildNow GG is about building fast cover while landing accurate shots. Gather materials, place walls, and edit openings to win duels.

Mix defensive building with quick peeks to control the fight.

Switch between weapons and builds smoothly to stay ahead of your opponent.

Controls

  • W / A / S / D — move and strafe
  • Mouse — aim and shoot
  • Build keys — place walls, ramps, and floors

Tips to Master BuildNow GG

Build cover before you reload so you do not get caught in the open.

Edit Angles
