Instructions

There Is No Game is a trickster puzzle where the interface itself is the challenge. Click, drag, and experiment to uncover hidden interactions.

Listen to the narrator, but do not always trust them. Each scene hides clues that unlock the next.

Take your time and test everything on screen. Sometimes the solution is to break the rules.
