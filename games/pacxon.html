How to Play PacXon?

Use the arrow keys to move PacXon. Fill up the screen by creating lines, but be careful not to let the ghosts touch your lines before they are complete. The game gets more challenging as you progress!

Who Created PacXon?

PacXon is inspired by the classic Pac-Man game and combines elements of both Pac-Man and Xonix.

Is PacXon Free and Unblocked?

Yes, PacXon is free to play and unblocked. Enjoy this classic game anytime, anywhere.

How does Pacxon support science standards?

Use Pacxon to explain momentum, friction, and cause/effect when turns go well or poorly.

Any family tie-ins for Pacxon?

Families can hold a "calmest hands" contest to see who keeps steady control the longest, then discuss strategies.

Is Pacxon safe to run on school laptops?

Yes—Pacxon loads locally and stores nothing beyond basic progress. A screenshot of the final score is all that’s needed.
