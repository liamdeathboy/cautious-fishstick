Instructions

Move with WASD or the arrow keys, and click to aim and smash; which action clicking performs depends entirely on which team you were randomly assigned to at the start of the match.

As a Hider, blend in by disguising as a normal-looking object in the environment and moving only when it is safe to do so, since movement is often what gives away a disguised player to an alert Smasher scanning the area.

As a Smasher, systematically checking objects that seem slightly out of place — an object that shouldn't be in that specific spot, or one that moved when nothing else did — is more effective than smashing objects at random across the whole map.

Controls

  • W / A / S / D — sprint around the map
  • Mouse — aim and throw props
  • Space — activate disguises or perks

Tips to Master Hide & Smash

Because a well-chosen disguise can look identical to a real object while stationary, it is usually movement — repositioning at the wrong moment while a Smasher is nearby — that actually reveals a Hider, so timing any repositioning for when Smashers are visibly occupied elsewhere is safer than moving whenever convenient.

Smashers should target anomalies, not smash indiscriminately

Since disguised Hiders are visually identical to real objects unless something is subtly wrong (a duplicate object, one in an unusual location), scanning for anomalies rather than clicking every object in reach finds Hiders faster and avoids wasting time on objects that were never a threat.

Key collection speed pressures both sides differently

Because Hiders win by collecting every key before being found, a Hider who prioritizes speed over caution risks revealing their position through movement, while a Smasher who searches too slowly gives Hiders more time to finish collecting — both roles are racing against the same clock from opposite directions.
