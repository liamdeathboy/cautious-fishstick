Instructions

Use the arrow keys to move Marisa and press Z to open or close doors; doors can be used both to pass through rooms and to block or slow down pursuing enemies.

Trampolines are how Marisa moves between the house's floors — she is unharmed while off the ground from a trampoline bounce, so timing movement around trampoline bounces is safer than trying to avoid enemies entirely at ground level.

Collecting the same item a second time while it is glowing multiplies its point value, so returning for a glowing repeat item is worth more than moving on to a fresh, non-glowing item of the same base value.

Tips to Master Marippy

Because Marisa cannot be harmed while airborne from a trampoline bounce, timing movement to be off the ground via a trampoline when Alice or a Shanghai doll is nearby avoids a life loss that staying grounded in the same spot would risk — trampolines double as both transit and temporary safety.

Doors control the chase, not just room access

Since doors can be closed behind Marisa to block or slow pursuers, using Z deliberately to shut a door after passing through — rather than only opening doors to move forward — turns the door mechanic into active crowd control against Alice and the Shanghai dolls chasing her.

Glowing repeat items are worth deliberately backtracking for

Because collecting the same item again while it glows multiplies its point value, a route that specifically loops back for a glowing repeat scores more than continuing forward to fresh items alone — recognizing a glowing item as a priority target rather than treating all items as equally worth collecting immediately maximizes score per run.
