Family & Classroom FAQ

Marippy Classroom FAQ

Answer the top questions families and admins raise about using Marippy during instruction.

How can Marippy support literacy lessons?

Treat every branch in Marippy as a text-dependent question. Students must cite dialogue or codex entries before the class votes on the next move.

What family prompt works with Marippy?

Send home a "What would we choose?" scenario so caregivers can discuss empathy, stakes, and character motivation together.

Is Marippy safe for classroom devices?

Marippy plays entirely in-browser with no accounts. Remind students not to share screenshots that include classmates’ names and to close the tab when done.
