Teacher Toolkit for Jetpack Joyride

Jetpack Joyride turns each dialogue choice into a close-reading exercise that spotlights classroom discussion norms, family prompts, and privacy-first play sessions.

Close-reading pause

Project a pivotal decision in Jetpack Joyride and have students cite the exact line that justifies their vote before advancing the story.

Family empathy prompt

Send home a one-sentence journal starter such as "What would our family choose in Jetpack Joyride, and why?" so caregivers can join the conversation.

Privacy-first storytelling

Jetpack Joyride runs fully in the browser with no logins; remind learners to close the tab when finished and avoid sharing screenshots that might reveal classmates’ choices.

Family & Classroom FAQ

Jetpack Joyride Classroom FAQ

Answer the top questions families and admins raise about using Jetpack Joyride during instruction.

How can Jetpack Joyride support literacy lessons?

Treat every branch in Jetpack Joyride as a text-dependent question. Students must cite dialogue or codex entries before the class votes on the next move.

What family prompt works with Jetpack Joyride?

Send home a "What would we choose?" scenario so caregivers can discuss empathy, stakes, and character motivation together.

Is Jetpack Joyride safe for classroom devices?

Jetpack Joyride plays entirely in-browser with no accounts. Remind students not to share screenshots that include classmates’ names and to close the tab when done.
