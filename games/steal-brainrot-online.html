Instructions

Left-click and drag a Brainrot character out of a base to pick it up, then release the mouse button to carry it back toward your own base — dropping it mid-carry leaves it available for anyone else to grab, including the player you took it from.

Because taken Brainrots can be re-stolen while still in transit back to your base, the walk back from a rival's base is exactly when a collected Brainrot is most at risk, not a safe victory lap.

Rarer Brainrot characters are worth more toward your overall collection value, so prioritizing which base to target based on visible rarity, rather than grabbing whatever is closest, produces a more valuable haul per successful steal.

Tips to Master Steal Brainrot Online

Because a stolen Brainrot can be re-stolen from you while you are still carrying it back, successfully lifting one from a rival's base is only half the job — planning the shortest safe route back to your own base matters as much as the initial grab, since a long exposed carry invites exactly the kind of theft you just performed.

Target rarity, not just proximity

Since rarer Brainrots contribute more to overall collection value than common ones, a steal aimed at the nearest available target regardless of rarity is a less efficient use of a risky trip than identifying and targeting a visibly rarer Brainrot even if it requires traveling farther.

Defending your own base matters as much as stealing from others

Because the same steal-and-carry mechanic that lets you take from rivals applies equally to your own collection, leaving a valuable base completely unguarded while off raiding elsewhere risks losing more from your own stock than a successful raid elsewhere gains.
