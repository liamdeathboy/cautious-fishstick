Instructions

Click a business's progress bar to earn cash manually and unlock the ability to buy more units of it; once a business has enough units, hiring a manager for it automates production so it no longer needs clicks.

Reinvesting profits into new businesses and upgrades grows total income faster than repeatedly clicking a single business, since later businesses generate dramatically more income per unit than earlier ones like the Lemonade Stand.

The Angel Investor system resets all current progress back to zero businesses in exchange for a permanent multiplier on all future earnings, so the highest-value play late in a run is often resetting deliberately rather than continuing to grind an already-slowing business empire.

Controls

  • Mouse — collect profits and buy managers
  • Hotkeys 1-9 — bulk purchase upgrades
  • Space — toggle speed-up boosts when available

Tips to Master Adventure Capitalist

Because businesses with managers keep earning while the game is closed, leaving the game idle for a stretch and returning to reinvest banked profits is a normal part of the intended pace — there is no benefit to leaving a browser tab open just to watch numbers increase in real time.

Later businesses outproduce earlier ones by orders of magnitude

Since each new business tier earns dramatically more per unit than the one before it, continuing to manually click and reinvest in an early business like the Lemonade Stand long after later businesses are available slows overall growth compared to redirecting that cash toward unlocking the next tier.

Angel Investor resets are a deliberate strategic choice

Because the Angel Investor system wipes all current business progress in exchange for a permanent multiplier on everything earned afterward, resetting too early forfeits growth that has not yet compounded, while waiting too long leaves multiplier gains unclaimed — timing the reset around when growth has visibly slowed is the actual skill involved.
