Instructions

Use the arrow keys to run, jump, and fly your duck through the cave, collecting coins along the way; unlike earlier Duck Life games, there is no separate training phase before a race — the run itself is the whole game.

Coins collected during runs fund gadgets and pets that boost your duck's abilities on future runs, so an early run focused on just surviving as long as possible to bank coins pays off on later runs with upgraded gear.

An arcade cabinet in the game offers 11 separate minigames, two of which are available from the start, and playing them raises your duck's IQ stat, which unlocks additional items in the in-game shop beyond what coins alone can buy.

Controls

  • Arrow keys — glide and dodge obstacles
  • Mouse — upgrade gear and pets
  • Space — use power-ups mid-flight

Tips to Master Duck Life 5

The earlier Duck Life games are built around training minigames that raise stats before a race; Duck Life 5 drops that structure entirely for continuous endless-runner gameplay, so strategies from the earlier games (training balance, stat grinding) do not carry over directly.

IQ from minigames unlocks shop items coins cannot buy

Because only 2 of the 11 arcade minigames are available at the start, and playing them raises your duck's IQ rather than earning coins directly, ignoring the arcade entirely locks you out of certain shop upgrades no matter how many coins you collect from runs alone.

Survival length compounds through upgrades

Coins from a longer run buy gadgets and pets that make the next run easier to extend further, so an early run's only goal should be banking as many coins as safely possible rather than attempting risky moves for a marginally higher score on that specific run.
