Instructions

Every round in Boxing Random throws a new arena twist at you—from icy floors to gravity-flipping rings—so stay light on your feet and watch your opponent’s balance before committing to a punch.

Feint with small hops to bait a swing, then unleash a straight punch or the infamous surprise kick when the distance is perfect. Remember: flying wildly often leaves you wide open for a counter.

Use the first seconds of each bout to read the round’s gimmick, then lean into short combos instead of button mashing. Precision beats chaos when the bell rings for overtime.

Controls

  • W / Arrow Up — jump and throw punches
  • E / Arrow Right — lean forward for a jab
  • Mobile Tap — tap the on-screen buttons to swing

Tips to Master Boxing Random

Every round in Boxing Random throws a new arena twist at you—from icy floors to gravity-flipping rings—so stay light on your feet and watch your opponent’s balance before committing to a punch.

Control the stumble

Mini hops reset your balance. Tap forward to close distance, then stop tapping before you swing so your fighter doesn’t tumble over its own gloves.

Capitalize on arena quirks

Snowy stages reward patient taps while windy rounds push punches farther. Adjust your rhythm to whatever wacky modifier the RNG dial spins up.

Bring a friend

Boxing Random shines in couch co-op. Trade controllers between rounds or pass a keyboard and see who adapts fastest to the next absurd arena rule.
