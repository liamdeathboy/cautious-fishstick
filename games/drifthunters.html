Instructions

Drift Hunters is the ultimate drifting game where you can customize cars, drift through tracks, and earn points to unlock new vehicles and upgrades. Test your drifting skills and dominate the leaderboard!

Teacher Toolkit

Teacher Toolkit for Drift Hunters

Drift Hunters turns each dialogue choice into a close-reading exercise that spotlights classroom discussion norms, family prompts, and privacy-first play sessions.

Close-reading pause

Project a pivotal decision in Drift Hunters and have students cite the exact line that justifies their vote before advancing the story.

Family empathy prompt

Send home a one-sentence journal starter such as "What would our family choose in Drift Hunters, and why?" so caregivers can join the conversation.

Privacy-first storytelling

Drift Hunters runs fully in the browser with no logins; remind learners to close the tab when finished and avoid sharing screenshots that might reveal classmates’ choices.

Family & Classroom FAQ

Drift Hunters Classroom FAQ

Answer the top questions families and admins raise about using Drift Hunters during instruction.

How can Drift Hunters support literacy lessons?

Treat every branch in Drift Hunters as a text-dependent question. Students must cite dialogue or codex entries before the class votes on the next move.

What family prompt works with Drift Hunters?

Send home a "What would we choose?" scenario so caregivers can discuss empathy, stakes, and character motivation together.

Is Drift Hunters safe for classroom devices?

Drift Hunters plays entirely in-browser with no accounts. Remind students not to share screenshots that include classmates’ names and to close the tab when done.
