Family & Classroom FAQ

MotoX3M Games Classroom FAQ

Answer the top questions families and admins raise about using MotoX3M Games during instruction.

How can MotoX3M Games support literacy lessons?

Treat every branch in MotoX3M Games as a text-dependent question. Students must cite dialogue or codex entries before the class votes on the next move.

What family prompt works with MotoX3M Games?

Send home a "What would we choose?" scenario so caregivers can discuss empathy, stakes, and character motivation together.

Is MotoX3M Games safe for classroom devices?

MotoX3M Games plays entirely in-browser with no accounts. Remind students not to share screenshots that include classmates’ names and to close the tab when done.
