Each round sends a new wave of zombies at you. Shoot them, knife them and survive until the round ends; every round after that brings more zombies that are faster and tougher. Every hit and kill earns points, which are the currency for everything else in the game.

Tips to Survive Black Ops Zombies

In the first few rounds, shoot each zombie several times before finishing it with your knife. Every bullet that hits earns points, so drawing out the early rounds gives you a big head start for doors, perks and the Mystery Box.

Buy Juggernog first

Juggernog lets you take more hits before going down, and nothing keeps a co-op run alive longer. Once you have it, add Speed Cola for faster reloads and Quick Revive so you can pick teammates up quickly.

Train zombies, do not get cornered

Run in a wide loop through open space so the horde follows you in a single group, then turn and fire into the pack. Getting trapped in a dead end or a narrow corridor is the most common way to go down in later rounds.

Split roles with your squad

Decide who holds each window or hallway and call out when you are reloading or moving. One player watching the doors while another trains zombies or hits the Mystery Box keeps the whole team alive much longer than everyone fighting on their own.

About Black Ops Zombies

Zombies is the co-op survival mode from Call of Duty: Black Ops, developed by Treyarch and released by Activision in 2010. It grew out of the surprise zombies mode in Call of Duty: World at War and became one of the most loved parts of the whole Call of Duty series.

The original game shipped with Kino der Toten, a haunted German theatre, and Five, set inside the Pentagon, and later map packs added Ascension, Call of the Dead, Shangri-La and Moon. Black Ops Zombies introduced classic weapons like the Thundergun, Winter's Howl and the Ray Gun returning from World at War, alongside the Mystery Box, Pack-a-Punch and Perk-a-Cola machines that have defined the mode ever since. Which maps you can play depends on the browser version hosted on vel.gg.

What makes Black Ops Zombies so replayable is that it never ends: there is no final round, just a horde that keeps getting stronger. Every game is a race to build up points, weapons and perks quickly enough to survive one more round, and with friends it turns into a team effort full of last-second revives and close calls.
