Teacher Toolkit

Teacher Toolkit for Snowline

Snowline turns each dialogue choice into a close-reading exercise that spotlights classroom discussion norms, family prompts, and privacy-first play sessions.

Close-reading pause

Project a pivotal decision in Snowline and have students cite the exact line that justifies their vote before advancing the story.

Family empathy prompt

Send home a one-sentence journal starter such as "What would our family choose in Snowline, and why?" so caregivers can join the conversation.

Privacy-first storytelling

Snowline runs fully in the browser with no logins; remind learners to close the tab when finished and avoid sharing screenshots that might reveal classmates’ choices.

Family & Classroom FAQ

Snowline Classroom FAQ

Answer the top questions families and admins raise about using Snowline during instruction.

How can Snowline support literacy lessons?

Treat every branch in Snowline as a text-dependent question. Students must cite dialogue or codex entries before the class votes on the next move.

What family prompt works with Snowline?

Send home a "What would we choose?" scenario so caregivers can discuss empathy, stakes, and character motivation together.

Is Snowline safe for classroom devices?

Snowline plays entirely in-browser with no accounts. Remind students not to share screenshots that include classmates’ names and to close the tab when done.
