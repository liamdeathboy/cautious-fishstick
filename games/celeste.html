Instructions

Celeste Classic is the original PICO-8 game that the full Celeste was later built from: a compact precision platformer about climbing a mountain one screen at a time, using a single air dash to reach ledges, cross gaps, and escape spikes that would otherwise be unreachable on a jump alone.

Your dash resets the moment you touch the ground, so most of the challenge comes from finding the exact jump-then-dash sequence a screen wants, rather than raw reflexes alone. Falling off the bottom of a screen or touching a hazard sends you back to the start of that screen, not the whole climb, so retrying costs seconds rather than progress.

Controls

  • Arrow keys — move and climb
  • Z or C — jump
  • X or V — dash in the direction you're holding (once per air time, until you touch ground)

Tips to Master Celeste Classic

Dash on the way up, not after you've started falling. Because the dash gives a burst of speed in whatever direction you're holding, triggering it near the peak of a jump extends your reach far more than using it once you're already dropping.

Touching ground refreshes everything

Since your one dash comes back the instant you land, a screen that looks impossible in the air is often solvable by finding a small ledge to briefly touch down on partway through.

Learn each screen, don't memorize a run

Because deaths only reset the current screen, treat every attempt as information about that one section rather than something to be anxious about losing progress over.
