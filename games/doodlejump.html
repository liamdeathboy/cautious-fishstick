Instructions

Doodle Jump is an addictive vertical platformer where you guide a character to jump higher and higher. Avoid obstacles, defeat enemies, and set new high scores!

Doodle Jump has become a classic arcade game, loved for its simple yet challenging gameplay. It has inspired countless players and remains a favorite for casual gaming.

Focus on timing your jumps and aiming for stable platforms. Avoid enemies and obstacles, and use power-ups strategically to boost your score. Practice to improve your reflexes!

Controls

  • Space — fire at monsters
  • Tap — shoot on touch screens

Teacher Toolkit for Doodle Jump

Doodle Jump turns each dialogue choice into a close-reading exercise that spotlights classroom discussion norms, family prompts, and privacy-first play sessions.

Close-reading pause

Project a pivotal decision in Doodle Jump and have students cite the exact line that justifies their vote before advancing the story.

Family empathy prompt

Send home a one-sentence journal starter such as "What would our family choose in Doodle Jump, and why?" so caregivers can join the conversation.

Privacy-first storytelling

Doodle Jump runs fully in the browser with no logins; remind learners to close the tab when finished and avoid sharing screenshots that might reveal classmates’ choices.

Family & Classroom FAQ

Doodle Jump Classroom FAQ

Answer the top questions families and admins raise about using Doodle Jump during instruction.

How can Doodle Jump support literacy lessons?

Treat every branch in Doodle Jump as a text-dependent question. Students must cite dialogue or codex entries before the class votes on the next move.

What family prompt works with Doodle Jump?

Send home a "What would we choose?" scenario so caregivers can discuss empathy, stakes, and character motivation together.

Is Doodle Jump safe for classroom devices?

Doodle Jump plays entirely in-browser with no accounts. Remind students not to share screenshots that include classmates’ names and to close the tab when done.
