Instructions

Sonic Advance brings classic 2D Sonic gameplay to the Game Boy Advance, with speed-focused zones full of loops, springs, and slopes designed to keep you moving as fast as possible rather than platforming carefully.

Each of the four playable characters moves differently — Tails can fly for short periods, Knuckles can glide and climb walls, and Amy uses her hammer for a different attack and jump style — so the same zone plays out differently depending on who you pick.

Collecting rings protects you from losing a life to a single hit, and gathering all seven Chaos Emeralds through special stages unlocks a transformation with even greater speed for whoever you're playing as.

Controls

  • Arrow keys — move / D-pad
  • Down + jump while moving — spin dash for a burst of speed
  • Z — jump
  • Enter — Start — pause the game

Tips to Master Sonic Advance

Pick a character to match the zone rather than sticking with one the whole game — Knuckles' climbing and Tails' flight open up different routes that a straight run as Sonic can't take.

Keep your ring count healthy in fast sections

Since high speed makes hazards harder to react to, a strong ring buffer forgives the occasional mistimed jump.

Chase special stages for Chaos Emeralds early

Because they're usually easier to access earlier in a playthrough, collecting Emeralds as you go is simpler than trying to backtrack for them later.
