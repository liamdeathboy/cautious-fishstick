Instructions

Doge Miner is an idle clicker where every click earns Dogecoins. Spend coins on shovels, workers, and machines to automate your mining.

Upgrades increase clicks per second, so balance manual clicking with long-term investments.

Keep an eye on the rocket meter and push for bigger milestones as your production grows.

Controls

  • Mouse / Touch — click to mine and buy upgrades
  • Upgrade panel — unlock tools, helpers, and boosts
  • Menu — adjust audio or reset progress

Tips to Master Doge Miner

Stack upgrades that boost coins per second, then add click boosts when prices spike.

Payback Planning

Choose upgrades that return their cost quickly so your mining rate stays climbing.

Automation Balance

Invest in workers early, then return to manual clicks for short bursts of growth.
