Instructions

Click the rock directly to earn one Dogecoin per click manually; this is the only income source at the very start, before any helpers have been purchased.

Spend earned Dogecoin on helpers, each of which adds passive Dogecoins-per-second income that continues generating currency without further clicks, similar in structure to other idle clickers like Cookie Clicker.

Because helpers scale your passive income while manual clicking stays a fixed one coin per click, the earlier you shift spending toward helpers instead of banking manual-click earnings, the faster your total income compounds over the course of a session.

Tips to Master Doge Miner

Since each click earns exactly one Dogecoin regardless of how the game has progressed, clicking never scales the way helper-based income does — the practical value of clicking drops sharply once even a few helpers are generating passive income each second.

Reinvest early rather than banking coins

Because helper income compounds over time (more helpers means more coins per second, which buys more helpers faster), spending Dogecoin on helpers as soon as they are affordable outpaces saving up coins for a single larger purchase later.

The moon is a long-term goal, not a quick one

The game's stated goal of reaching the moon requires substantial passive income built up over an extended session, so treating early clicking and helper purchases as a warm-up phase rather than the main event matches how the game's progression is actually paced.

About Doge Miner

Doge Miner is an idle clicker game built around the Doge meme and the Dogecoin cryptocurrency, released in 2014 at the height of the Dogecoin craze. You play the famous Shiba Inu, swinging a pickaxe at a rock to mine Dogecoins one click at a time.

Coins buy helpers that mine for you automatically, and upgrades make both your clicks and your helpers more productive. The running joke behind the game is the crypto slogan "to the moon": as your mining operation grows, the goal shifts from a single rock on Earth to reaching the moon. Like Cookie Clicker, it is easy to start, satisfying to watch grow, and built to be left running in a background tab.

Doge Miner FAQ

Is Doge Miner real crypto mining?

No. Doge Miner is just a game about the Dogecoin meme. Nothing is mined for real and no cryptocurrency or wallet is involved.

How do I earn coins faster in Doge Miner?

Spend early coins on helpers rather than saving them. Helpers earn coins every second without clicking, so their income quickly overtakes manual mining.

Is Doge Miner like Cookie Clicker?

Yes. It uses the same idle-clicker formula: click for currency, buy producers that earn automatically, and keep reinvesting to grow faster.
