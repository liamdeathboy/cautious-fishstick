Instructions

Five Nights at Epstein is a horror survival game where you play as a night security guard. Your goal is to survive five nights by monitoring security cameras and managing your limited power supply.

Watch the cameras carefully to track movement in the building. Use the lights and doors strategically to protect yourself, but be careful — every action drains your power!

Stay calm, manage your resources wisely, and survive until 6 AM. Each night becomes progressively more challenging, testing your nerves and strategic thinking.

Controls

  • Mouse — navigate cameras, toggle lights and doors
  • Click — interact with security systems
  • Space — toggle camera view

Tips to Master Five Nights at Epstein

Conserve power by only using lights and doors when absolutely necessary. Learn the patterns of movement and plan your strategy accordingly.

Game Strategy

Monitor cameras frequently but don't keep them open constantly — this drains power. Listen for audio cues that indicate nearby threats.

Survival Tactics

Stay calm under pressure. Panic leads to wasted power and mistakes. Time your door and light usage carefully to maximize survival chances.

Player Guide

Survival Guide for Five Nights at Epstein

Master the art of survival with these essential strategies for surviving all five nights.

Power Management

Power is your most valuable resource. Only use lights and doors when you detect immediate threats. Keeping cameras open also consumes power.

Camera Strategy

Check cameras in a systematic pattern. Learn which areas are most critical and monitor them frequently while leaving others for periodic checks.

Audio Cues

Listen carefully for audio cues that signal movement and threats. Sound often provides early warnings before visual confirmation on cameras.
