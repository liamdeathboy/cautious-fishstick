Instructions

The title screen frames every session the same way: earn as much money as you can in today's challenge. That daily framing is the point of the scoring - you are not working through a campaign towards an ending, you are trying to beat a number, and the run resets so you can immediately try a better line through the same territory.

Play is driven with the mouse or by tapping, so it works on a school Chromebook and a phone without any change. Bandits are what pay, so the loop is to find them, deal with them, and bring the bounty home rather than exploring the landscape for its own sake.

The world is rendered as untextured low-poly geometry in a flat red and tan palette. That is a size decision rather than a stylistic accident - with no room for texture files in 13 kilobytes, the terrain is built from coloured polygons, which is why silhouettes and shape read more clearly here than surface detail does.

Controls

Tips to Master Backcountry

Backcountry runs on goodluck, the piesku team's own template for tiny browser games, which they publish openly for anyone to clone and build on. That is worth knowing because it explains the game's shape: goodluck supplies a game loop and a rendering pipeline and deliberately not much else, so what you are playing is close to raw WebGL with the fat cut away to fit the byte limit.

The daily challenge is the replay hook

Because a session is scored in money earned rather than progress made, the game is designed around short repeated attempts. Knowing where bandits tend to appear is worth more than reflexes, and the second or third run through a given day's challenge is usually worth several times the first, which is the intended curve rather than a sign you played badly the first time.

Hats, web monetization and a third-place finish

js13kGames 2019 ran a separate monetization category using the Web Monetization standard, and Backcountry's entry into it let supporters pick different hats for their character - a cosmetic reward rather than a gameplay advantage. It took third in that category. The cosmetic unlocks are inactive here, but nothing about the core bounty hunting depends on them.
