Instructions

Space Invaders puts you in control of a single cannon sliding left and right along the bottom of the screen, firing straight up at a grid of aliens marching steadily downward and sideways as a group.

Destructible cover blocks sit between you and the aliens, offering protection from incoming fire but eroding with every shot that hits them — including your own — so relying on the same patch of cover the whole game isn't a safe long-term plan.

The alien formation speeds up as you destroy more of them, since fewer aliens still move at the same group pace, meaning the final few invaders of a wave are usually the fastest and most dangerous.

Controls

  • Left / Right arrows or A / D — move your cannon
  • Space — fire

Tips to Master Space Invaders

Clear columns evenly rather than focusing one side. Leaving a full column of aliens on one side means dealing with that entire column's fire alone as the group speeds up.

Preserve some cover for the endgame

Since the last few aliens move fastest and are hardest to dodge, having intact cover left late in a wave matters more than it did early on.

Watch for the bonus ship

A UFO occasionally crosses the top of the screen for bonus points — worth a shot if you have a clean line, but not worth abandoning your position over.
