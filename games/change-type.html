Instructions

Change Type is a platformer built around a single mechanic: your character has a type that can be switched, and the color-coded blocks scattered through each level react differently depending on whether your current type matches them.

Reading a room before committing to a type is the real skill — a block that's safe to stand on or pass through in one type can be dangerous in another, so most levels are solved by figuring out the right sequence of type switches rather than by movement alone.

Controls

  • Arrow keys — move and jump
  • Number keys or on-screen icons — switch your current type

Tips to Master Change Type

Check a block's color before you touch it, not after. Since the wrong type turns a safe-looking block into a hazard, get in the habit of confirming your current type matches what's ahead before you jump onto it.

Plan switches ahead of the jump

Because switching type takes a beat, changing type mid-air after you've already committed to a jump is riskier than switching while you're still standing on solid ground.

Retrace your steps mentally after a death

Most deaths come from one mismatched switch rather than a movement mistake, so look at which type you were in right before you died rather than assuming the jump itself was the problem.
