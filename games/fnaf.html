Instructions

Open the monitor to check security cameras across the building, and use the flashlight to illuminate the Main Hall and the two side air vents, since animatronics approach through those three routes and need to be spotted before they reach the office.

Because there are no doors in this game, an animatronic that reaches the office cannot be locked out; putting on the Freddy Fazbear head fools most animatronics into leaving you alone, but it does not work on Withered Foxy, who has to be scared off with the flashlight instead.

Periodically flip to the Prize Counter camera and rewind the music box; if it fully unwinds while you are not watching it, the Puppet escapes its box and becomes an active threat, so the music box needs regular attention on top of monitoring the halls and vents.

Controls

  • Mouse — check cameras and close doors
  • Space — toggle the flashlight in hallways
  • Ctrl — mute the call quickly

Tips to Master FNAF

Since nothing can physically block an animatronic from entering once it reaches the office, the entire defense depends on spotting it on the monitor or in a vent light while it is still approaching — reacting only after it is already in the office is too late for most animatronics.

The Freddy head has one exception

Wearing the Freddy Fazbear head fools most animatronics that enter the office into leaving without attacking, but Withered Foxy ignores it entirely and has to be driven off with the flashlight specifically — treating the head as a universal defense against every animatronic is a common way to lose to Foxy specifically.

The music box is a background timer, not a one-time task

Because the music box unwinds continuously in the background regardless of what else is happening, forgetting to check the Prize Counter camera while dealing with a different threat can let it run out and release the Puppet — treating it as a recurring check rather than something to handle once and forget is necessary to survive a full night.
