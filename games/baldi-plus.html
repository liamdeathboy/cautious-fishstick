Tips to Master Baldi's Basics Plus

Listen for Baldi's ruler and plan your routes before you sprint. Each floor is generated on the fly, so mark safe corridors mentally and grab items that counter every teacher.

Decode every faculty member

Baldi isn't alone. Principal of the Thing nabs you for running, Mrs. Pomp eats time with pop quizzes, and Beans blocks halls with gum. Learn their audio cues and keep counters like safety scissors handy.

Master the random events

Plus Mode introduces daily modifiers—darkness, slippery floors, or surprise guests. Check the chalkboard before each run so you're not caught off guard when Baldi changes the rules mid-lesson.

Speedrun the elevators

You only escape by banking enough notebooks to unlock the elevator. Route efficiently, save stamina for the final chase, and stash a B-Soda to knock Baldi back just before the doors close.

Family & Classroom FAQ

Baldi's Basics Plus Classroom FAQ

Answer the top questions families and admins raise about using Baldi's Basics Plus during instruction.

How can Baldi's Basics Plus support literacy lessons?

Treat every branch in Baldi's Basics Plus as a text-dependent question. Students must cite dialogue or codex entries before the class votes on the next move.

What family prompt works with Baldi's Basics Plus?

Send home a "What would we choose?" scenario so caregivers can discuss empathy, stakes, and character motivation together.

Is Baldi's Basics Plus safe for classroom devices?

Baldi's Basics Plus plays entirely in-browser with no accounts. Remind students not to share screenshots that include classmates’ names and to close the tab when done.
