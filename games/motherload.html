Instructions

Use the arrow keys or WASD to fly the pod and dig into dirt; the pod cannot dig straight upward, so descending routes have to account for that restriction when planning how to return to the surface.

Collect minerals by flying over them or digging them out directly with the drill, then return to the surface to sell everything collected for money spent on upgrades like fuel capacity and drill speed.

Press I to open the inventory, C to detonate Plastic Explosives that clear a large area around the pod at once, and Q to activate the Quantum Teleporter, which instantly returns the pod to the surface in an emergency.

Tips to Master Motherload

Because the pod cannot tunnel directly upward, digging straight down into a dead end without a clear diagonal or looping path back can strand the pod underground — planning a route with a viable return path in mind, not just the fastest way down, avoids getting stuck.

Fuel and cargo capacity gate how deep a single trip can go

Since both fuel and cargo space are limited until upgraded, a trip that goes too deep too early can run out of fuel before reaching the surface again or fill cargo space with low-value minerals that crowd out room for more valuable finds — upgrading fuel and cargo early makes each subsequent trip meaningfully more productive.

The Quantum Teleporter is an emergency exit, not a routine tool

Because the Quantum Teleporter instantly returns the pod to the surface, saving it for situations where continuing to dig risks losing the pod or its cargo entirely — rather than using it as a convenient shortcut on every trip — preserves it for the moments it actually matters most.

About Motherload

Motherload is a mining game set on Mars, released by XGen Studios in 2004 and one of the best-loved Flash games ever made. You pilot a small digging pod for a mining company, drilling deeper and deeper underground to collect valuable minerals and bring them back to the surface to sell.

Every trip is a trade-off between greed and survival. Your fuel tank and cargo hold are limited, falls damage your hull, and the deeper you go the more valuable the ore and the more dangerous the heat, gas pockets and lava. Money buys better drills, engines, hulls, fuel tanks and radiators at the surface shops. A strange story about the company's boss unfolds as you dig, leading to a final encounter deep underground.

Motherload FAQ

What should I upgrade first in Motherload?

A bigger fuel tank and a stronger drill. More fuel lets you stay underground longer, and a faster drill gets you to the valuable layers before your tank runs low.

How do I avoid running out of fuel in Motherload?

Watch the fuel gauge and head back up while you still have about a third of a tank left. Buying a Quantum Teleporter gives you an emergency escape to the surface.

Is Motherload still playable without Flash?

Yes. Schplay runs the original game through the Ruffle Flash emulator, so it works in a modern browser without the Flash plugin.
