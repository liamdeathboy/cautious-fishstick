Family & Classroom FAQ

Arcade Wizard Classroom FAQ

Answer the top questions families and admins raise about using Arcade Wizard during instruction.

How can Arcade Wizard support literacy lessons?

Treat every branch in Arcade Wizard as a text-dependent question. Students must cite dialogue or codex entries before the class votes on the next move.

What family prompt works with Arcade Wizard?

Send home a "What would we choose?" scenario so caregivers can discuss empathy, stakes, and character motivation together.

Is Arcade Wizard safe for classroom devices?

Arcade Wizard plays entirely in-browser with no accounts. Remind students not to share screenshots that include classmates’ names and to close the tab when done.
