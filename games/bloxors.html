Instructions

Bloxors is a challenging puzzle game where you roll a block through a maze to reach the goal. Test your logic and problem-solving skills!

Controls

  • Arrow keys — roll the block around the platform
  • Space — switch between linked blocks when available
  • R — restart the current level
