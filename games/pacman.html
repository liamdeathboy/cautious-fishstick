How to play Pac-Man?

Use the arrow keys to control Pac-Man. Navigate through the maze, eat all the pellets, and avoid the ghosts. Eating large pellets will allow you to eat the ghosts for a short time.

Who created Pac-Man?

Pac-Man was created by Namco.

Is Pac-Man free and unblocked?

Yes, Pac-Man is free to play and can be accessed on various platforms.

Teacher Toolkit for Pacman

Pacman rewards sequencing and perseverance, making it a natural fit for classroom goal-setting, family storytelling, and privacy cues.

Checkpoint retros

After a tricky jump in Pacman, have students outline the three steps they took to succeed.

Family walkthroughs

Encourage students to narrate one favorite level from Pacman to a family member, emphasizing grit.

Private progress logs

Because Pacman saves locally, have learners jot progress in journals instead of posting it online.

Family & Classroom FAQ

Pacman Classroom FAQ

Answer key classroom, family, and privacy questions for Pacman.

What learning benefit comes from Pacman?

Pacman makes students break goals into micro-steps, a skill that transfers to writing and math.

How can families connect to Pacman?

Have students describe one obstacle course to caregivers and brainstorm coping strategies.

Is Pacman okay for privacy policies?

Pacman runs without accounts and never asks for chat handles, so it fits tight privacy rules.
