Family & Classroom FAQ

1v1.lol Classroom FAQ

Answer the top questions families and admins raise about using 1v1.lol during instruction.

How can 1v1.lol support literacy lessons?

Treat every branch in 1v1.lol as a text-dependent question. Students must cite dialogue or codex entries before the class votes on the next move.

What family prompt works with 1v1.lol?

Send home a "What would we choose?" scenario so caregivers can discuss empathy, stakes, and character motivation together.

Is 1v1.lol safe for classroom devices?

1v1.lol plays entirely in-browser with no accounts. Remind students not to share screenshots that include classmates’ names and to close the tab when done.
