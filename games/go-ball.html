Instructions

Go Ball works on a simple rule: you draw a line with the mouse, and the ball follows it as a physical track, rolling and falling according to gravity rather than teleporting along your drawing.

Each level places the ball and the goal with obstacles in between, so the puzzle is sketching a path that gets the ball safely from one to the other — a line that looks reasonable can still fail if it sends the ball into a wall or off the edge of the level.

Controls

  • Mouse (click and drag) — draw the path the ball will roll along
  • Release the mouse / confirm — let the ball go and watch it follow your line

Tips to Master Go Ball

Draw smooth curves, not sharp angles. A sharply angled line tends to stop or deflect the ball unpredictably, while a gentle curve keeps its momentum going the direction you intended.

Use gravity instead of fighting it

Because the ball is affected by gravity throughout, a path that lets it roll naturally downhill toward the goal usually works better than one that tries to push it uphill.

Redraw rather than patch a failed attempt

Since a small change to an early section of the line can change everything that happens afterward, it's often faster to start the drawing over than to tweak a path that's already gone wrong.
