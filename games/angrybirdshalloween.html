Instructions

Click and drag a bird backward on the slingshot to set launch angle and power, then release to fire it at structures made of wood, glass, and stone that shelter pigs; the goal on every level is destroying every pig, not just the structure around them.

Different bird types have different in-flight abilities — some split into multiple birds, others accelerate sharply — activated with a tap or click once airborne, so choosing when to trigger a bird's special ability changes how effectively it clears a specific structure.

Materials react differently to impact: glass shatters easily under a direct hit, wood needs more force or a well-placed hit to break through, and stone resists all but the heaviest impacts, so matching a bird's trajectory and power to the material actually in the way matters more than aiming at the pig directly.

Controls

  • Mouse / Touch — drag to set sling angle and power
  • Space — activate bird abilities mid-flight
  • R — reset the current level

Tips to Master Angry Birds Halloween

Because each bird type's in-flight ability suits certain structures better than others — a splitting bird against multiple separated targets, an accelerating bird against a single tough barrier — activating the wrong ability at the wrong moment wastes a shot that could have cleared far more of the level.

Material matters more than raw impact force

Since glass, wood, and stone each have different resistance to impact, a shot that would shatter glass instantly may barely dent stone, so reading which material is actually blocking the path to a pig — rather than launching at maximum power regardless of material — produces more efficient level clears.

This is a themed reskin of the core Angry Birds formula, not a different game

Because the Halloween theme changes visuals and level dressing rather than the underlying slingshot-and-structure mechanics, skills built playing the standard Angry Birds formula transfer directly here — the challenge comes from new level layouts, not from any different core mechanic.
