Instructions

In Gun Runner, look one obstacle ahead instead of reacting at the last second. Early positioning gives you better choices and fewer risky corrections.

Use boosts and jump actions only when they open a clear route. In Gun Runner, wasting movement tools usually costs more than a short slowdown.
