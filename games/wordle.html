Instructions

Guess a five-letter word and press Enter. Each tile then changes colour - green means the letter is correct and in the right position, yellow means the letter is in the word but somewhere else, and grey means it is not in the word at all. You have six guesses.

Open the settings menu with the gear icon to pick your mode. Daily gives everyone the same word once per day, Hourly rotates a new word every hour, and Infinite lets you play back-to-back with no waiting - which is the mode most people actually want when they have twenty minutes rather than one puzzle's worth of attention.

Hard Mode, also in settings, forces you to reuse every hint you have found: any green letter must stay in place and any yellow letter must appear somewhere in your next guess. It removes the common tactic of burning a guess on five fresh letters to gather information, and it is the setting to turn on once ordinary Wordle stops being difficult.

Tips to Master Wordle+

The game carries two separate dictionaries: roughly 2,300 possible answers and about 10,650 words it will accept as valid guesses. That split is inherited from the original design and it is genuinely useful - it means obscure words like AALII are legal to guess for their letters even though they will never be the answer, so you can probe with unusual letter combinations without wasting a turn on something the game rejects outright.

Opening words are about letter coverage

A good opener is not a likely answer, it is a wide net. Words such as CRANE, SLATE and AUDIO score well because they cover common consonants and vowels without repeating a letter, and a repeated letter effectively wastes one of your five slots on information you already have. The strongest practical habit is to spend guesses one and two on ten distinct common letters, then solve with the remaining four.

Streaks, stats and settings that stay put

The game tracks your current and maximum streak alongside a distribution of how many guesses your wins took, kept separately for each mode. A high-contrast colourblind palette swaps the green and yellow for colours that stay distinguishable, and a dark theme is on by default. All of it is stored in your browser, so it persists between visits on the same machine but does not follow you to another one.

Yellow tiles carry more information than they look like they do

A yellow tile tells you two facts, not one: the letter is in the word, and it is not in that position. Players routinely use the first half and forget the second, then waste a guess putting the same letter back in a square already ruled out. Duplicate letters are the other common trap - if you guess a word with two Es and the answer has one, the game marks one E and greys the other, which reads as contradictory until you know the rule. Treating grey as 'not here again' rather than 'not in the word' when the letter appears twice will save a guess more often than any clever opening word.
