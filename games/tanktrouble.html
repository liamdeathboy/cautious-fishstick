Instructions

Tank Trouble is an exciting tank battle game where you navigate your tank and shoot enemies. Use your skills to achieve high scores and progress through challenging levels!

Controls

  • Arrow keys — drive and aim (player 1)
  • Q / W / E — control tank for player 2
  • I / J / K — controls for player 3 in local matches
