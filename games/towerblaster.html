Instructions

Tower Blaster is a strategic tower building game where you compete to build a stable tower faster than your opponent. Plan your moves and outsmart your competition!

Controls

  • Mouse — swap blocks with the tower
  • Space — discard unwanted bricks
  • H — toggle hints

Tips to Master Tower Blaster

Get better at Tower Blaster with short, focused sessions: learn the timing, study each mistake, and push for a new personal best every run.

How to Play Tower Blaster?

Use your mouse to select and place blocks. Strategically arrange blocks to build a stable tower and beat your opponent!
