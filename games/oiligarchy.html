Oiligarchy Classroom FAQ

How does Oiligarchy support instruction?

Students analyze resource spikes and bottlenecks in Oiligarchy, then defend which upgrades or defenses to prioritize—a perfect formative assessment.

How can families join in without extra accounts?

Send home a "What would you build next?" screenshot so caregivers can compare strategies using the same privacy-friendly browser version.

Does Oiligarchy protect student privacy?

Oiligarchy plays locally in the browser, so no usernames or birthdates are collected. Remind students not to share save files in public chats.
