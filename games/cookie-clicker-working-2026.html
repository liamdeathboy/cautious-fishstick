Instructions

Cookie Clicker Working 2026 is an idle incremental game where every click bakes cookies. Click the big cookie to earn your first batch, then spend cookies on cursors, grandmas, and factories.

Upgrades boost cookies per click and cookies per second, so check the store often and keep your production running.

Golden cookies appear randomly and deliver short bursts of power. Click them quickly to speed up your run.

Controls

  • Mouse / Touch — click the cookie and buy upgrades
  • Golden cookies — click for temporary boosts
  • Menu — adjust audio or reset progress

Tips to Master Cookie Clicker Working 2026

Balance cheap buildings with multipliers that boost your overall output. Early gains snowball into faster growth.

Upgrade Timing

Save for upgrades that multiply production once you have a steady base of cookies per second.

Automation Strategy

Prioritize buildings that increase cookies per second, then circle back to click upgrades for bursts of speed.
