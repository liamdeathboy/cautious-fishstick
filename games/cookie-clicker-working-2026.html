Instructions

Click the giant cookie in the center of the screen to earn cookies manually, then spend them in the store on buildings like Cursors, Grandmas, Farms, and Factories — each building type produces a fixed number of cookies per second on its own, and buying more of the same building increases in price each time.

Golden Cookies appear periodically and float across the screen; clicking one before it disappears triggers a random temporary bonus such as a production multiplier or a lump sum of cookies, so keep an eye on the screen even while buildings are producing passively.

Later buildings unlock minigames tied to specific buildings — the Grimoire (Wizard Towers), the Pantheon (Temples), the Garden (Farms), and the Stock Market (Banks) — each with its own separate mechanics that add extra ways to boost production beyond simply buying more buildings.

Tips to Master Cookie Clicker Working 2026

Each building purchase increases that building's price for the next one, but total cookies-per-second from a full building lineup outpaces manual clicking almost immediately after the first few purchases. Prioritizing whichever building currently gives the best cookies-per-second for its price beats save-and-click strategies.

Golden Cookies reward attention, not idling

Because Golden Cookies appear at random intervals and vanish if not clicked in time, a run played with the tab in the background misses bonuses that a run with the tab open and visible would catch — the random multiplier or cookie windfall from a Golden Cookie can be worth more than several minutes of normal production.

Minigames unlock progressively, not all at once

The Garden, Grimoire, Pantheon, and Stock Market minigames each require owning enough of their linked building (Farms, Wizard Towers, Temples, and Banks respectively) before they become available, so ignoring a building type entirely also means permanently skipping the strategic options its minigame would unlock.

About Cookie Clicker

Cookie Clicker is the game that defined the idle genre. It was created by French developer Julien "Orteil" Thiennot and released as a browser game in 2013. You start by clicking a giant cookie, then spend cookies on Cursors, Grandmas, Farms, Mines, Factories and much stranger buildings that bake cookies for you.

Numbers grow from single cookies to quintillions and beyond, and the game keeps adding layers as you go: upgrades, achievements, Golden Cookies with temporary bonuses, minigames like the Garden and the Stock Market, and Ascension, which resets your bakery in exchange for permanent Heavenly Chips. Underneath the jokes, Cookie Clicker is a surprisingly deep game about compounding growth and smart spending.

Cookie Clicker FAQ

Who made Cookie Clicker?

Cookie Clicker was created by Julien Thiennot, known as Orteil, and first released in 2013.

Does Cookie Clicker save my progress?

The game autosaves in your browser. You can also export your save from the Options menu as a backup and import it later on another device.

When should I ascend in Cookie Clicker?

Many players ascend for the first time once they can earn a few hundred prestige levels, since each level permanently increases cookie production in future runs.
