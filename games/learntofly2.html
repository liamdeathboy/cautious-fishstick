Instructions

Use the arrow keys (or switch to mouse steering in options) to control glide angle in flight, and hold Space to fire rockets once purchased, same as the original game's core controls.

Payloads, new to this entry, help destroy obstacles that would otherwise block or damage your flight path, while sleighs improve how much speed and height you carry off the initial launch ramp — both are upgrades purchased with coins earned from distance.

Choose between Classic mode (rack up the highest distance within a limited number of days), Arcade mode (fly through rings and avoid obstacles for points), or Story mode (complete a series of challenges to unlock new items), since each mode scores and rewards progress differently.

Controls

  • Left / Right — steer the penguin in the air
  • Space — activate sled boosts
  • 1-3 — trigger gadgets

Tips to Master Learn To Fly 2

Since the original Learn to Fly had no way to clear an obstacle directly in the flight path other than avoiding it, payloads give this sequel a direct counter — timing a payload's use for an obstacle that would otherwise end the flight early is a strategy the first game simply did not support.

Mode choice changes what actually counts as progress

Because Classic, Arcade, and Story track completely different objectives — raw distance under a day limit, ring-and-obstacle scoring, or challenge completion — grinding coins in one mode does not necessarily translate to progress in another; picking a mode based on the specific goal for that session matters more than always defaulting to one.

Sleigh upgrades pay off before the glide even starts

Because sleighs improve the launch itself rather than mid-flight control, their benefit is front-loaded into the first few seconds of a run — a strong sleigh upgrade can make an otherwise mediocre glide angle produce a longer distance than a perfect glide launched from a weak takeoff.
