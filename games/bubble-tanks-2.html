Instructions

Bubble Tanks 2 puts you in control of a small round tank exploring an open arena filled with enemy bubbles of increasing size and firepower. Destroying them drops experience orbs, and collecting enough orbs levels your own tank up into a new, more powerful form with a different shape and weapon layout.

Each level-up is a genuine evolution rather than a simple stat boost — your tank's silhouette changes and its weapons change with it, so part of the game is discovering which of the branching tank forms suits how you like to fight.

Because bigger enemy bubbles hit harder and take more shots to pop, sizing up a fight before engaging matters: chasing every enemy on screen the moment you see it is a fast way to get overwhelmed before you've leveled up enough to handle it.

Controls

  • W / A / S / D or Arrow keys — move your tank
  • Mouse — aim
  • Left click — fire

Tips to Master Bubble Tanks 2

Clear small bubbles first. They're weaker but still drop orbs, so farming the easy targets in an area is usually a safer way to level up than diving straight at the biggest bubble on screen.

Retreat when you're under-leveled

Since enemy bubbles hit harder than your current form can comfortably trade with, backing off to fight in a less crowded part of the arena is often better than standing and trading shots you'll lose.

Pay attention at every evolution

Because each new tank form changes your weapon and movement, take a moment after leveling up to notice how your firing pattern has changed before diving back into a fight the same way you did with your previous form.
