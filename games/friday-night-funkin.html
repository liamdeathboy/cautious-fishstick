Instructions

Friday Night Funkin' (FNF) is a rhythm-based music game where you battle opponents in epic rap battles. Hit the right notes to win and impress your girlfriend!

FNF has gained massive popularity for its catchy music, unique art style, and challenging gameplay. It has inspired a vibrant modding community and countless fan creations.

Focus on timing your key presses to match the beat. Practice difficult sections to improve accuracy and aim for high scores. Stay calm and enjoy the music!

Controls

  • W / A / S / D — alternate controls for lefty players
  • Enter — pause and swap songs

Family & Classroom FAQ

Friday Night Funkin Classroom FAQ

Answer the top questions families and admins raise about using Friday Night Funkin during instruction.

How can Friday Night Funkin support literacy lessons?

Treat every branch in Friday Night Funkin as a text-dependent question. Students must cite dialogue or codex entries before the class votes on the next move.

What family prompt works with Friday Night Funkin?

Send home a "What would we choose?" scenario so caregivers can discuss empathy, stakes, and character motivation together.

Is Friday Night Funkin safe for classroom devices?

Friday Night Funkin plays entirely in-browser with no accounts. Remind students not to share screenshots that include classmates’ names and to close the tab when done.
