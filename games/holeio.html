Instructions

Hole.io is a competitive multiplayer game where players control a black hole, devour objects, and grow larger to dominate the arena. Outsmart opponents and become the biggest hole!

Hole.io has become a popular game for its unique mechanics and engaging multiplayer gameplay. It has inspired players to strategize and compete in a fun and dynamic environment.

Focus on devouring smaller objects to grow quickly. Avoid larger holes and plan your movements strategically to outmaneuver opponents and dominate the arena.

Controls

  • Mouse / Touch — drag the hole around the city
  • Space — recenter on your hole instantly
  • Tab — check leaderboard standings

Teacher Toolkit for Hole.io

Hole.io turns each dialogue choice into a close-reading exercise that spotlights classroom discussion norms, family prompts, and privacy-first play sessions.

Close-reading pause

Project a pivotal decision in Hole.io and have students cite the exact line that justifies their vote before advancing the story.

Family empathy prompt

Send home a one-sentence journal starter such as "What would our family choose in Hole.io, and why?" so caregivers can join the conversation.

Privacy-first storytelling

Hole.io runs fully in the browser with no logins; remind learners to close the tab when finished and avoid sharing screenshots that might reveal classmates’ choices.

Family & Classroom FAQ

Hole.io Classroom FAQ

Answer the top questions families and admins raise about using Hole.io during instruction.

How can Hole.io support literacy lessons?

Treat every branch in Hole.io as a text-dependent question. Students must cite dialogue or codex entries before the class votes on the next move.

What family prompt works with Hole.io?

Send home a "What would we choose?" scenario so caregivers can discuss empathy, stakes, and character motivation together.

Is Hole.io safe for classroom devices?

Hole.io plays entirely in-browser with no accounts. Remind students not to share screenshots that include classmates’ names and to close the tab when done.
