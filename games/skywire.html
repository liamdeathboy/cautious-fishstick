Instructions

Skywire puts a small cable car on a wire strung across each level, and rather than steering it directly, you control how it swings and when it releases from one wire segment to travel to the next.

Gems are placed along and off the direct path, so timing your swing to reach ones slightly out of the way — without releasing at the wrong moment and losing your car to a fall — is the main puzzle of each level.

Controls

  • Mouse click (hold) — swing on the current wire segment
  • Release — let go to fly toward the next wire or landing point

Tips to Master Skywire

Release at the top of a swing, not the bottom — like any momentum-based swing, letting go near the peak sends you further than releasing while falling.

Don't chase every gem

Some gems sit in positions that only make sense to grab if you're already swinging that direction — reaching for an awkward one can cost you the whole run.

Watch the next wire segment before you release

Since your car needs to actually reach the next attachment point, glancing ahead before letting go avoids releasing into empty air.
