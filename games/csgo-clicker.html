Tips to Master CSGO Case Opener

Because the currency, cases, and skins here exist only within this fan-made clicker, nothing pulled or earned transfers to an actual Steam or CS:GO account — the game recreates the feeling of case-opening rather than functioning as a real trading or marketplace tool.

Rarity weighting means volume beats saving for one big open

Since each case roll is independently weighted toward common outcomes, spreading currency across many cheaper case opens generally returns more total value over a long session than saving up for a single expensive case, simply because more independent rolls average out closer to the case's true expected value.

Selling low-value pulls is what sustains the loop

Because opening cases costs currency that has to come from somewhere, consistently selling common pulls rather than holding onto them is what funds the next round of case openings — hoarding low-value skins without selling them stalls progress toward affording higher-tier cases.
