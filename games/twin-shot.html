Instructions

Twin Shot has you guiding a small bird across floating platforms suspended in a hazy sky, jumping between them while firing at ground-based enemies and threats that stand between you and the next safe ledge.

Because platforms float at different heights with gaps between them, timing a jump and a shot together matters more than in a straightforward shooter — clearing a threat before you land often decides whether the next jump is safe.

Controls

  • Arrow keys or WASD — move and jump between platforms
  • Mouse or attack key — fire at enemies

Tips to Master Twin Shot

Clear a platform before you commit to landing on it — jumping in blind is riskier than firing ahead first, even if it costs a moment of momentum.

Use height to your advantage

Firing down at enemies from a higher platform is generally safer than engaging them at the same level, where they can hit back more easily.

Don't rush the climb

Since falling from a floating platform usually costs progress, moving deliberately from ledge to ledge beats sprinting upward and misjudging a jump.
