Instructions

Pick a specific shop from the hub first, since each game (Pizzeria, Pancakeria, Cupcakeria, and the rest) has its own recipe steps and stations, even though they all share the same basic order-build-serve structure.

Head to the Order Station to see what a waiting customer wants — toppings, cook time, how it should be cut or finished — then move through the shop's specific stations (assembling, cooking, finishing) using drag, swipe, and tap actions with the mouse.

Matching the order exactly, including cook time and any extra requests, earns a bigger tip than rushing through with a roughly-correct result, so checking the order details again before serving matters more than speed alone.

Controls

  • Mouse — prepare orders and multi-task stations
  • Space — flip or serve certain items

Tips to Master Papa's Games

Because every game in the hub follows the same order-prepare-serve loop, switching from one shop to another feels familiar immediately, but the exact stations and timing (a pizza's bake time versus a sundae's scoop-and-topping order) differ enough that assuming identical steps across shops leads to mistakes on orders that need shop-specific handling.

Exact order matching pays more than rough approximation

Since tips scale with how precisely an order matches what the customer actually asked for, serving something close but not exact — a slightly overcooked pizza, a topping placed in the wrong spot — earns less than taking the extra moment to check the order ticket again before finishing.

Juggling multiple customers is the real time-management skill

Because customers keep arriving while an earlier order is still being prepared, managing several orders across different stations at once — rather than fully finishing one customer before starting the next — is what the later, busier parts of any of these games actually test.
