Instructions

Papa's Games is a series of restaurant management games where players take on the role of a chef and manager. Cook delicious meals, serve customers, and grow your restaurant empire.

Papa's Games have been a staple in the casual gaming world, known for their engaging gameplay and charming graphics. They have inspired players to develop time management and multitasking skills.

Focus on customer satisfaction by preparing orders accurately and quickly. Upgrade your restaurant to improve efficiency and attract more customers.

Teacher Toolkit

Teacher Toolkit for Papa's Games

Papa's Games turns each dialogue choice into a close-reading exercise that spotlights classroom discussion norms, family prompts, and privacy-first play sessions.

Close-reading pause

Project a pivotal decision in Papa's Games and have students cite the exact line that justifies their vote before advancing the story.

Family empathy prompt

Send home a one-sentence journal starter such as "What would our family choose in Papa's Games, and why?" so caregivers can join the conversation.

Privacy-first storytelling

Papa's Games runs fully in the browser with no logins; remind learners to close the tab when finished and avoid sharing screenshots that might reveal classmates’ choices.

Family & Classroom FAQ

Papa's Games Classroom FAQ

Answer the top questions families and admins raise about using Papa's Games during instruction.

How can Papa's Games support literacy lessons?

Treat every branch in Papa's Games as a text-dependent question. Students must cite dialogue or codex entries before the class votes on the next move.

What family prompt works with Papa's Games?

Send home a "What would we choose?" scenario so caregivers can discuss empathy, stakes, and character motivation together.

Is Papa's Games safe for classroom devices?

Papa's Games plays entirely in-browser with no accounts. Remind students not to share screenshots that include classmates’ names and to close the tab when done.
