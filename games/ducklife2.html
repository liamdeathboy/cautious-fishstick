Teacher Toolkit for Duck Life 2

Duck Life 2 turns each dialogue choice into a close-reading exercise that spotlights classroom discussion norms, family prompts, and privacy-first play sessions.

Close-reading pause

Project a pivotal decision in Duck Life 2 and have students cite the exact line that justifies their vote before advancing the story.

Family empathy prompt

Family & Classroom FAQ

Duck Life 2 Classroom FAQ

Answer the top questions families and admins raise about using Duck Life 2 during instruction.

How can Duck Life 2 support literacy lessons?

Treat every branch in Duck Life 2 as a text-dependent question. Students must cite dialogue or codex entries before the class votes on the next move.

What family prompt works with Duck Life 2?

Send home a "What would we choose?" scenario so caregivers can discuss empathy, stakes, and character motivation together.

Is Duck Life 2 safe for classroom devices?

Duck Life 2 plays entirely in-browser with no accounts. Remind students not to share screenshots that include classmates’ names and to close the tab when done.
