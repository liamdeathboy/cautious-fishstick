Instructions

Henry Stickman is an interactive adventure game where players make choices to guide Henry through hilarious and unpredictable scenarios. Solve puzzles and enjoy the story-driven gameplay!

The Henry Stickman series has become a cult classic for its humor, engaging storylines, and creative gameplay. It has inspired countless fans and remains a favorite among adventure game enthusiasts.

Stickman Games Classroom FAQ

Answer the top questions families and admins raise about using Stickman Games during instruction.

How can Stickman Games support literacy lessons?

Treat every branch in Stickman Games as a text-dependent question. Students must cite dialogue or codex entries before the class votes on the next move.

What family prompt works with Stickman Games?

Send home a "What would we choose?" scenario so caregivers can discuss empathy, stakes, and character motivation together.

Is Stickman Games safe for classroom devices?

Stickman Games plays entirely in-browser with no accounts. Remind students not to share screenshots that include classmates’ names and to close the tab when done.
