Instructions

Drag tiles down from the tray at the top of the screen and drop them onto the map to build a continuous road connecting the left edge to the right. Tiles snap to the isometric grid, and a placement that cannot legally connect is refused rather than silently accepted, so an invalid drop costs you nothing.

You are not limited to road. Water, forest and helipad tiles appear as the puzzles progress, each with its own rule - forest tiles award bonus points, and the helipad lets you stack a tile aside for later rather than committing it immediately. Long-press any tile you have already placed to bulldoze it and free that square up again.

Puzzle mode runs through a sequence of named levels including Roads 101, Town Planning, Reverse the Flow, The Block Forest, Dual Carriageway and Little Condo by the Sea, each introducing one new idea. Completing enough of them unlocks Free Map mode from the main menu, where there is no objective and you simply build a town for its own sake.

Controls

  • Drag from the top tray — pull a tile down and place it on the map
  • Click / tap — select and position tiles
  • Long press — bulldoze a tile you no longer want
  • Camera button — export the current map as a screenshot

Tips to Master Road Blocks

Tiles arrive in a fixed sequence and you have to take them as they come, so the puzzle is less about where the road should go than about what you can afford to do with the piece currently in your hand. Placing a straight where a corner is coming is how most levels are lost, and the helipad exists precisely so you can park an awkward tile instead of forcing it somewhere damaging.

Bulldozing is part of the loop, not a penalty

Long-pressing to demolish a tile is not an admission of failure - several levels are difficult to solve without it, because committing early to a route and then adapting is cheaper than trying to plan the entire path before you know which tiles are coming. If a level tells you that you are stuck, the fix is almost always removing one badly-placed tile rather than restarting.

Free Map is the reward, and it is worth unlocking

The mode you unlock by clearing puzzles removes the win condition entirely and gives you the full tile set to build with. There is a camera button that exports whatever you have made as an image file, which in 2015 was an unusually generous feature to spend precious bytes on and remains the reason people still share screenshots of towns built in this game.
