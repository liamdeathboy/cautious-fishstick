Instructions

Mr. Mine is an addictive idle mining game where players dig deep into the earth, discover treasures, and expand their mining empire. Manage resources and unlock upgrades to maximize your profits.

Mr. Mine has been a favorite among idle game enthusiasts for its engaging mechanics and endless progression. It has inspired players to strategize and optimize their mining operations.

Focus on upgrading your tools and hiring workers to increase efficiency. Explore deeper levels to find rare resources and maximize your earnings.

Controls

  • Mouse — assign workers and collect resources
  • Hotkeys 1-6 — buy drill upgrades quickly
  • Space — toggle auto-battle for monsters

Teacher Toolkit

Teacher Toolkit for Mr Mine

Mr Mine turns each dialogue choice into a close-reading exercise that spotlights classroom discussion norms, family prompts, and privacy-first play sessions.

Close-reading pause

Project a pivotal decision in Mr Mine and have students cite the exact line that justifies their vote before advancing the story.

Family empathy prompt

Send home a one-sentence journal starter such as "What would our family choose in Mr Mine, and why?" so caregivers can join the conversation.

Privacy-first storytelling

Mr Mine runs fully in the browser with no logins; remind learners to close the tab when finished and avoid sharing screenshots that might reveal classmates’ choices.

Family & Classroom FAQ

Mr Mine Classroom FAQ

Answer the top questions families and admins raise about using Mr Mine during instruction.

How can Mr Mine support literacy lessons?

Treat every branch in Mr Mine as a text-dependent question. Students must cite dialogue or codex entries before the class votes on the next move.

What family prompt works with Mr Mine?

Send home a "What would we choose?" scenario so caregivers can discuss empathy, stakes, and character motivation together.

Is Mr Mine safe for classroom devices?

Mr Mine plays entirely in-browser with no accounts. Remind students not to share screenshots that include classmates’ names and to close the tab when done.
