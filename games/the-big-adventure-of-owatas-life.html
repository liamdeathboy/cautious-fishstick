Instructions

Use the arrow keys to move, Z to jump, and X to fire the Owata Buster forward at enemies; movement and the buster are the entire toolkit for getting through each ASCII-art stage.

Any contact with an enemy or environmental trap kills Jinsei Owata instantly, so the game is built around dying repeatedly to learn a stage's hidden traps and enemy patterns rather than surviving on a health bar.

Press R to reset and retry immediately after a death, since fast, frequent restarts are how the game expects you to memorize a stage's specific dangers through trial and error rather than reading ahead or avoiding death entirely.

Tips to Master The Big Adventure of Owata's Life

Because any hit kills instantly and ASCII-art stages often hide traps in ways that are not visually obvious in advance, expecting to clear a new section without dying at least once is unrealistic — the intended approach is dying to discover a trap's location, then using that knowledge on the next attempt.

Fast resets are core to the intended pace

Since pressing R restarts immediately rather than requiring a long respawn sequence, the game is built around rapid repeated attempts — treating each death as a quick information-gathering pass rather than a frustrating setback matches how the game's trial-and-error design is meant to be played.

ASCII art can disguise both traps and safe paths

Because stages are built from ASCII art rather than conventional tile graphics, a visually ambiguous symbol can be either a deadly trap or a harmless safe passage with no obvious visual distinction — testing ambiguous-looking sections cautiously (accepting the near-certain death) is how their true nature actually gets revealed.
