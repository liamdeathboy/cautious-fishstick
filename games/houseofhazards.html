Instructions

Player 1 moves with A/D (or the left/right arrow keys) to turn, W (or up) to jump, and S (or down) to crouch; Player 2 uses J and L to turn, I to jump, and K to crouch. A third and fourth player need a gamepad, using the left stick to move and the face buttons to jump and duck.

Tasks around the house — like the coffee machine or the mailbox — are what you are racing to complete, and finishing one before your rivals is how you score, so beelining for the nearest active task usually beats exploring the whole house.

Hazards like the swinging hammer or the toaster's flying toast trigger automatically as players walk near them and will hit whoever is closest regardless of which player caused it, so a hazard that just fired is briefly safer to walk past than one that hasn't triggered yet.

Controls

  • W / A / S / D — move player characters
  • Space — interact with traps or furniture
  • Arrow keys — control trap triggers when spectating

Tips to Master House Of Hazards

Several hazards, like swinging hammers and thrown objects, pass at head height, which means crouching (S for Player 1, K for Player 2) clears them when jumping would put you directly in the hazard's path. Reflexively jumping at every hazard is a common mistake — check whether the hazard is low or high before reacting.

Hazards reset on a timer, not on demand

A hazard that just triggered needs a moment before it can fire again, so the instant after a hammer swings or toast launches is the safest window to walk past that specific spot. Waiting out an active hazard's reset is usually faster than detouring around it.

Race the task, not the other player

Since completing your assigned task is what scores points, chasing down an opponent to block them costs time you could spend finishing your own task, especially in 1-player mode against computer opponents that path directly to their goal without hesitation.
