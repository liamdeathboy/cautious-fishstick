Instructions

Blumgi Rocket is a physics-driven platformer where each stage is a bite-size puzzle. Use your rocket boost to hop between platforms, dodge hazards, and land on the target pad.

Levels reward calm aim and small adjustments. A tiny shift in angle changes your arc, so experiment and watch how momentum carries you.

Start with short hops to line up the next landing, then commit to longer launches when the route feels safe.

Controls

  • Mouse / Touch — aim your launch and release to boost
  • Restart icon — reset the level for another attempt
  • Pause menu — adjust audio or exit the run

Tips to Master Blumgi Rocket

Favor shorter boosts to keep your angle under control, then extend your arc once you are lined up. Each landing is a checkpoint, so use them to recalibrate.

Momentum Planning

Watch how far your rocket drifts after a boost. If you overshoot, lower the angle and reduce your launch power to land softly.

Level Strategy

Scan the stage for safe platforms before you launch. Chaining two small hops is often safer than a single long jump.
