Instructions

Territorial.io starts you with a small home territory on a large map shared with many AI and player-controlled nations, and your goal is simply to grow — claiming adjacent land expands your borders, but every new tile needs enough army strength behind it to hold.

Population and army size are linked: more territory and cities generate more population over time, which can be converted into army strength to both defend what you have and push into new land, so growth and military strength reinforce each other rather than competing directly.

Because it's a persistent shared map, a session can run for a long time as small nations get absorbed by larger ones and borders shift, so long-term positioning — not just an aggressive early rush — often decides who ends up controlling the most land.

Controls

  • Mouse — select adjacent territory to expand into
  • Mouse scroll / drag — zoom and pan around the map

Tips to Master Territorial.io

Expand into weaker, undefended neighbors before trying to challenge a strong one. Easy territory grows your population and army faster than a costly fight against a well-defended nation.

Don't overextend your borders

A large territory with thin army coverage everywhere is more vulnerable than a smaller one that's solidly defended, since any weak point can be exploited by a neighboring nation.

Watch for larger nations consolidating nearby

Since bigger players can absorb smaller ones quickly, keeping an eye on which neighboring nation is growing fastest helps you decide whether to expand away from them or fortify against them.
