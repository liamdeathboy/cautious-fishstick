Instructions

Move the mouse to steer your cell in that direction; there is no keyboard movement, so positioning is entirely mouse-driven while Space and W handle the two special actions.

Press Space to split your cell into two smaller ones that can combine again after a cooldown — splitting lets a piece of your cell lunge forward past a smaller opponent that would otherwise outrun you, but it also leaves both halves temporarily weaker until they recombine.

Press W to eject a small piece of your own mass as a moving pellet, which is mainly used to feed a teammate's cell or to bait a much larger opponent's cell away from you rather than to attack directly.

Controls

  • Mouse — steer your cell across the map

  • W — eject mass to feed allies or fire viruses

Tips to Master Agar.io

A split sends one half of your cell shooting forward in the mouse's current direction, which is what actually lets you catch a smaller cell that would otherwise simply outrun your unsplit mass. The tradeoff is that both halves are smaller and vulnerable until the recombine cooldown finishes, so splitting next to a larger cell is dangerous.

Bigger cells move slower

Cell speed drops as mass increases, so a much larger cell chasing you directly will rarely catch up on a straight line — the real danger is a large cell cutting off your path or trapping you against the edge of the map, not a head-on chase.

Ejecting mass has uses beyond feeding

While W is mainly known for feeding mass to a teammate's cell to help them grow past a threshold, ejected mass can also be used to bait a large predator cell into moving in a specific direction, buying time to escape toward open space instead.
