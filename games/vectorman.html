Instructions

Vectorman is a run-and-gun action platformer: you move and jump through hazardous levels while firing at waves of robotic enemies, picking up different weapon types that change your firing pattern and effectiveness against different foes.

Certain sections let you transform into a vehicle form — like a drill for tunneling through material or other vehicle modes for specific obstacles — temporarily changing your abilities to solve a problem the normal platforming moveset can't handle alone.

Levels combine straightforward combat with environmental hazards like toxic sludge and crushing machinery, so reading a room for hazards is just as important as handling the enemies in it.

Controls

  • Arrow keys — move / D-pad
  • Z — jump
  • X — fire your current weapon
  • Enter — Start — pause the game

Tips to Master Vectorman

Try out a new weapon pickup before committing to it in a tough fight — different weapon types suit different enemy patterns, and the one you're already using isn't always the best fit.

Use vehicle transformations for their intended obstacle

Sections that offer a transformation are usually built around needing that specific form, so switching back to normal too early can leave you stuck.

Watch the environment as much as the enemies

Toxic terrain and crushing hazards can do as much damage as an enemy attack, so treat the level itself as part of the threat, not just background scenery.
