Instructions

Super Meat Boy is built entirely around fast retries: levels are short, hazards are instantly lethal, and dying just drops you back at the start of the same level within a second, so momentum comes from attempts, not caution.

Wall-jumping and precise mid-air direction changes are core to clearing most levels, and later stages expect you to chain several of these moves together without a single mistake.

Controls

  • Arrow keys / WASD — move
  • Spacebar — jump
  • Hold direction toward a wall — wall-jump

Tips to Master Super Meat Boy

Treat every death as information, not a setback — the instant respawn is designed for rapid trial and error, so the fastest way through a level is usually just attempting it repeatedly until the pattern clicks.
