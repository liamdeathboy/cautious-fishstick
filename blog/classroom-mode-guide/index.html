Classroom Mode Guide for Teachers

February 14, 2026 5 min read By Team Schplay

How to build curated playlists, share links safely, and keep sessions focused.

1. Pick a clear session goal

Choose one objective for each session: reaction speed, puzzle solving, or strategy planning. Keeping the goal narrow helps students focus.

2. Build a short playlist

  1. Select 3 to 5 games with similar difficulty and controls.
  2. Open each game in advance to confirm loading and sound settings.
  3. Keep one backup game ready in case a network filter blocks a page.

3. Define time boundaries

Use a fixed time window such as 10 or 15 minutes. This keeps sessions predictable and makes transitions easier.

Tip: Share one page that links your playlist instead of sending multiple URLs in chat.

4. Capture feedback quickly

At the end of class, ask what game felt most engaging and why. Those responses are useful for deciding the next week’s playlist.
