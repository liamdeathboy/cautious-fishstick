Weekly Update Post Template

February 10, 2026 3 min read By Team Schplay

Use this format for your weekly recap so players can skim changes fast.

What shipped this week

  • List 2 to 5 meaningful updates only.
  • Link to any new pages or features.
  • Mention fixes that users have been waiting on.

What is improving next

Share one short roadmap section with upcoming priorities. Keep this realistic and avoid overpromising.

Community shoutouts

Include player feedback highlights, top comments, or questions that shaped this week’s direction.

Tip: Reuse the same structure each week so readers can quickly find updates.
