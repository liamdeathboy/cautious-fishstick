Suggest a Strategy Game

Teacher Toolkit

  • Use tower defense or management games to discuss budgeting, resource allocation, and long-term planning.
  • Have students write persuasive essays defending their strategy or analyzing an opponent’s moves.
  • Connect strategy titles to social studies by examining cause-and-effect, diplomacy, or the impact of choices.
  • Prompt reflection: what data did you track, and how did it inform your next decision?

Curriculum Connections

Math & Economics

Idle games and management sims highlight percentages, ROI, and systems thinking.

Reading & Writing

Story-driven choices encourage summarizing, predicting outcomes, and understanding cause-and-effect.

Science & STEM

Strategy games demonstrate feedback loops, energy transfer, and the design process—great for STEM tie-ins.

Strategy FAQ

How do I keep students on task?

Set clear goals: reach level X, write down three decisions, or capture a screenshot of resource charts.

Can these support SEL?

Yes. Strategy games promote patience, empathy, and reflection—essential social-emotional skills.
