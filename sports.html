Pitch a Sports Game

Teacher Playbook

  • Use quick sports challenges as energizers between lessons. Have students reflect on reaction time, angles, or teamwork.
  • Ask learners to calculate speed, distance, or score differentials to link games with math and science standards.
  • Create stations: one for physical vocabulary (dribble, velocity), one for sports strategy discussions, and one for writing highlights.
  • Encourage students to design their own practice drills after playing—great for health and PE reflections.

Curriculum Connections

Math & Physics

Track shot angles, bounces, or acceleration to discuss measurement, geometry, and force.

Literacy & SEL

Have students write play-by-play recaps, practice sports journalism, or discuss sportsmanship scenarios.

Health & Strategy

Games can model decision making, reaction time, and practice planning—use them to brainstorm real-life drills.

Sports Games FAQ

How can PE teachers use Schplay?

Project a game as a whole-class reaction drill or use laptops for strategy planning before moving outside.

What about accessibility?

Many titles rely on single-key input. Pair students or provide adaptive controllers so everyone participates.
