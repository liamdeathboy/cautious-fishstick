Schplay Privacy Policy

Schplay provides free, classroom-ready activities designed to help teachers introduce logic, reading, and STEM skills through interactive games. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit schplay.com.

Information We Collect

  • Limited analytics data. We measure page views, device types, and general geography using privacy-aware analytics so teachers understand what resources are popular.
  • Voluntary contact details. If you email us (for example, to request a resource for your class), we store your name and address solely to respond.
  • Advertising identifiers. Google AdSense uses cookies and similar technologies to deliver relevant ads and report aggregate performance. You can manage preferences at adssettings.google.com.

How We Use Data

  • Operate and improve our library of learning games.
  • Customize recommended activities for teachers and families.
  • Detect abuse and keep the site safe for students.
  • Serve contextual Google AdSense ads that help keep the site free.

Cookies & Google AdSense

Cookies help us remember basic settings (such as dark/light theme) and allow AdSense to provide relevant, brand-safe advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits. You may opt out of personalized ads through Google’s Advertising Policy or by using any industry opt-out tools.

Children & Classroom Safety

Schplay’s content is curated for teachers and students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Educators who create shared devices should review browser privacy settings and supervise usage.

Your Choices

  • Access / Delete. Email us to review, update, or delete any voluntary information you’ve provided.
  • Cookie controls. Adjust browser settings to disable cookies, or use built-in tools like Chrome’s “Clear browsing data.”
  • Advertising preferences. Manage interest-based ads through Google Ad Settings or other self-regulatory opt-out pages.

Data Security

We use HTTPS, limited-access storage, and periodic security reviews. While no system can be perfectly secure, we continually monitor for issues and respond quickly.

Changes

We may update this policy as we expand our learning catalog or adopt new tools. Major changes will be noted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Email privacy@schplay.com with any privacy questions or teacher-specific requests.
